## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Bariatric Surgery when the criteria in this policy (MP-0455) are met.
The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 43644.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
documented failure of at least six weeks of conservative management.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that the requested
frequency exceeds the limit stated in the policy. The determination is issued within 60
calendar days of receipt for a post-service request.

## Administration and Review

production.
