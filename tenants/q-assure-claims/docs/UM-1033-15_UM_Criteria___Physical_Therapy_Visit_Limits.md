## Review Criteria

Utilisation management criteria applied to requests under medical policy MP-1033 (Physical
Therapy Visit Limits), procedure code 97110.

Approve where the submitted documentation establishes documented failure of at least six
weeks of conservative management.

Refer to clinical review where the documentation is incomplete. Deny where the member was
not eligible on the date of service.

Determinations must be issued within 30 calendar days of receipt for a pre-service request
in accordance with 45 CFR 162.1602.
