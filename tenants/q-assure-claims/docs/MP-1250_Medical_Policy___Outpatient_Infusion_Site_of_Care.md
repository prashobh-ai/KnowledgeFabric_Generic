## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Outpatient Infusion Site of Care when the criteria in this policy
(MP-1250) are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 96365.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
the absence of a contraindication recorded in the submitted notes.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that the requested
frequency exceeds the limit stated in the policy. The determination is issued within 72
hours for an expedited request.

## Administration and Review

This policy is owned by the Utilisation Management Lead and reviewed at least annually, or
sooner where clinical evidence, coding or regulation changes.

Revisions are approved by the Compliance Officer and configured in the medical policy
library. Configuration is validated against a regression set before release to production.

Regulatory basis: 42 CFR 405.926 — initial determinations and the appeals process.
