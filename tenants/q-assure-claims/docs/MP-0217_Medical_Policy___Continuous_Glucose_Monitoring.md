## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Continuous Glucose Monitoring when the criteria in this policy
(MP-0217) are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 95250.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
documented failure of at least six weeks of conservative management.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that prior authorisation
was not obtained before the service was rendered. The determination is issued within 15
calendar days where an extension has been applied and notified.
