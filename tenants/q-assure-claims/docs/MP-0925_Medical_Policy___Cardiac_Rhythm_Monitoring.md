## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Cardiac Rhythm Monitoring when the criteria in this policy (MP-0925)
are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 93298.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
documented failure of at least six weeks of conservative management.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that the diagnosis
submitted does not support medical necessity for the procedure billed. The determination is
issued within 72 hours for an expedited request.

## Administration and Review

This policy is owned by the VP Claims Operations and reviewed at least annually, or sooner
where clinical evidence, coding or regulation changes.

Revisions are approved by the Medical Policy Director and configured in the provider portal.
Configuration is validated against a regression set before release to production.

Regulatory basis: 42 CFR 422.566 — organisation determinations and notification
requirements.
