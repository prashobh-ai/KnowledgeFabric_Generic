## Review Criteria

Utilisation management criteria applied to requests under medical policy MP-1148 (Skilled
Nursing Facility Admission), procedure code 99304.

Approve where the submitted documentation establishes a documented clinical assessment
within the preceding 90 days.

Refer to clinical review where the documentation is incomplete. Deny where the member was
not eligible on the date of service.

Determinations must be issued within 30 calendar days of receipt for a pre-service request
in accordance with 42 CFR 422.568.
