## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Advanced Imaging for Low Back Pain when the criteria in this policy
(MP-0142) are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 72148.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes a
documented clinical assessment within the preceding 90 days.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that the documentation
submitted did not establish that conservative therapy was attempted. The determination is
issued within 30 calendar days of receipt for a pre-service request.

## Administration and Review

This policy is owned by the Revenue Integrity Manager and reviewed at least annually, or
sooner where clinical evidence, coding or regulation changes.

Revisions are approved by the VP Claims Operations and configured in the enrolment and
eligibility system. Configuration is validated against a regression set before release to
production.
