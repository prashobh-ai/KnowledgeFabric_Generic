## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Physical Therapy Visit Limits when the criteria in this policy
(MP-1033) are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 97110.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
evidence that a lower cost site of care was considered and excluded.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that prior authorisation
was not obtained before the service was rendered. The determination is issued within 30
calendar days of receipt for a pre-service request.

## Administration and Review

This policy is owned by the Payment Integrity Lead and reviewed at least annually, or sooner
where clinical evidence, coding or regulation changes.

Revisions are approved by the Compliance Officer and configured in the claims adjudication
platform. Configuration is validated against a regression set before release to production.
