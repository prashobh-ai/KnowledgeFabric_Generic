## Coverage Criteria

Q-Assure Claims covers Spinal Fusion for Degenerative Disease when the criteria in this
policy (MP-0631) are met. The primary procedure code addressed by this policy is 22633.

The service is considered medically necessary when the submitted documentation establishes
documented failure of at least six weeks of conservative management.

Where the criteria are not met, the request is denied on the basis that the service was
performed at a site of care not covered under the benefit. The determination is issued
within 30 calendar days of receipt for a pre-service request.
