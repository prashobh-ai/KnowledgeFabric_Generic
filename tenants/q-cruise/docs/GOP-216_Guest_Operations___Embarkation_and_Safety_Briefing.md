## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Solstice and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the guest
services platform and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the crew member has not completed the associated familiarisation, embarkation is
paused and the HSQE Manager is notified before the vessel next sails.

Requirement basis: ISM Code Section 9 — reports and analysis of non-conformities and
accidents.
