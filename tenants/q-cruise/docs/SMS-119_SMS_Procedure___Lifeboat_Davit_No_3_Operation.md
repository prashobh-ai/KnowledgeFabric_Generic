# SMS Procedure — Lifeboat Davit No.3 Operation

**Document number:** SMS-119  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Anita Delacroix (Director of Marine Operations, STF-6350)  
**Approved by:** Tomas Eriksen (Staff Captain, STF-5290)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Zenith.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Lifeboat Davit
No.3 rests with the Director of Marine Operations.

Compliance is required under ISM Code Section 9 — reports and analysis of non-conformities
and accidents.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Lifeboat Davit No.3 is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, do not operate and
raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the electronic logbook.
4. Report any a corroded securing arrangement noted on visual inspection to the Staff
Captain at the next scheduled review.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
