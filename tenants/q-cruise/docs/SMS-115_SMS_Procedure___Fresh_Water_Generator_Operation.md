# SMS Procedure — Fresh Water Generator Operation

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Marina.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Fresh Water
Generator rests with the Master.

Compliance is required under MLC 2006 Regulation 3.1 — accommodation and recreational
facilities.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Fresh Water Generator is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the equipment shows evidence of damage or leakage, do not operate and raise a
defect record.
3. Record the check in the fleet management system.
4. Report any a maintenance record completed without the verifying signature to the Chief
Engineer at the next scheduled review.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
