# SMS Procedure — Main Engine No.1 Operation

**Document number:** SMS-113  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Anita Suleiman (HSQE Manager, STF-7405)  
**Approved by:** Elena Rahimi (Fleet Technical Superintendent, STF-2845)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Aurora.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Main Engine No.1
rests with the HSQE Manager.

Compliance is required under ISM Code Section 10 — maintenance of the ship and equipment.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Main Engine No.1 is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the crew member has not completed the associated familiarisation, do not operate
and raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the guest services platform.
4. Report any a temperature log with a gap over the preceding period to the Fleet Technical
Superintendent at the next scheduled review.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
