# SMS Procedure — Ballast Water Treatment System Operation

**Document number:** SMS-120  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Neha Delacroix (Safety Officer, STF-1700)  
**Approved by:** Clara Solberg (Fleet Technical Superintendent, STF-3486)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-08-08  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Celeste.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Ballast Water
Treatment System rests with the Safety Officer.

Compliance is required under ISM Code Section 7 — development of plans for shipboard
operations.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Ballast Water Treatment System is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the equipment shows evidence of damage or leakage, do not operate and raise a
defect record.
3. Record the check in the electronic logbook.
4. Report any a temperature log with a gap over the preceding period to the Fleet Technical
Superintendent within 14 days.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
