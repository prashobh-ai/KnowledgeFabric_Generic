# SMS Procedure — Auxiliary Generator No.2 Operation

**Document number:** SMS-118

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Meridian.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Auxiliary
Generator No.2 rests with the Hotel Operations Manager.

Compliance is required under SOLAS Chapter III Regulation 19 — emergency training and
drills.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Auxiliary Generator No.2 is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, do not operate and
raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the safety reporting database.
4. Report any a maintenance record completed without the verifying signature to the
Designated Person Ashore before the vessel next sails.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
