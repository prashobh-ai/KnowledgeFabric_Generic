## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Aurelia and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the safety
reporting database and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the required certificate is not valid, embarkation is paused and the Chief Engineer is
notified before the next port of call.

Requirement basis: MLC 2006 Regulation 3.1 — accommodation and recreational facilities.
