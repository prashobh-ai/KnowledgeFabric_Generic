# SMS Procedure — Bow Thruster Operation

**Document number:** SMS-117  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Jonas Calloway (Fleet Technical Superintendent, STF-1049)  
**Approved by:** Karl Marchetti (Designated Person Ashore, STF-9542)  
**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Solstice.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Bow Thruster
rests with the Fleet Technical Superintendent.

Compliance is required under SOLAS Chapter II-2 — fire protection, detection and extinction.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Bow Thruster is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the equipment shows evidence of damage or leakage, do not operate and raise a
defect record.
3. Record the check in the planned maintenance system.
4. Report any an obstruction to an escape route noted during rounds to the Designated Person
Ashore at the next scheduled review.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
