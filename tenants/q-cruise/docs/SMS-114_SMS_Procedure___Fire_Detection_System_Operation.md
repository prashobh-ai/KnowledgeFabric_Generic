# SMS Procedure — Fire Detection System Operation

**Document number:** SMS-114  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Pieter Bertrand (Designated Person Ashore, STF-4538)  
**Approved by:** Ingrid Trelawney (Safety Officer, STF-8151)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Horizon.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Fire Detection
System rests with the Designated Person Ashore.

Compliance is required under ISM Code Section 7 — development of plans for shipboard
operations.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Fire Detection System is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the crew member has not completed the associated familiarisation, do not operate
and raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the guest services platform.
4. Report any an obstruction to an escape route noted during rounds to the Safety Officer
within 14 days.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
