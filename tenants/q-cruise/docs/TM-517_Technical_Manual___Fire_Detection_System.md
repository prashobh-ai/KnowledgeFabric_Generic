## Operating Limits and Maintenance

Operating and maintenance information for the Fire Detection System as fitted to MV
Q-Horizon and sister vessels.

Do not operate where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval.
Operation outside the stated limits may result in damage and invalidate the maker's
warranty.

Planned maintenance intervals are stated in the maker's schedule and are reflected in the
electronic logbook.
