## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Meridian and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the crew
training record system and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the crew member has not completed the associated familiarisation, embarkation is
paused and the Staff Captain is notified before the vessel next sails.

Requirement basis: MARPOL Annex IV — prevention of pollution by sewage from ships.
