# SMS Procedure — Main Engine No.1 Operation

**Document number:** SMS-121  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Tomas Ashgrove (Hotel Operations Manager, STF-5419)  
**Approved by:** Lucia Delacroix (Fleet Technical Superintendent, STF-1395)  
**Revision:** C  
**Effective:** 2026-09-09  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Aurora.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Main Engine No.1
rests with the Hotel Operations Manager.

Compliance is required under MARPOL Annex IV — prevention of pollution by sewage from ships.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Main Engine No.1 is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, do not operate and
raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the crew training record system.
4. Report any an expired calibration label on a monitoring instrument to the Fleet Technical
Superintendent before the vessel next sails.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
