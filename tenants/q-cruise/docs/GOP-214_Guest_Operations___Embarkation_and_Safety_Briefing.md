## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Marina and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the
electronic logbook and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the crew member has not completed the associated familiarisation, embarkation is
paused and the Master is notified at the next scheduled review.

Requirement basis: SOLAS Chapter II-2 — fire protection, detection and extinction.
