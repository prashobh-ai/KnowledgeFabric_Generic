# SMS Procedure — Galley Ventilation Hood Operation

**Document number:** SMS-116  
**Document type:** Safety Management System Procedure  
**Owner:** Karl Haverford (HSQE Manager, STF-5397)  
**Approved by:** Clara Okonjo (Director of Marine Operations, STF-5747)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Purpose and Responsibility

This procedure forms part of the safety management system of Q-Cruise and applies to all
vessels in the fleet, including MV Q-Aurelia.

The Master retains overriding authority. Day-to-day responsibility for the Galley
Ventilation Hood rests with the HSQE Manager.

Compliance is required under ISM Code Section 7 — development of plans for shipboard
operations.

## Procedure and Records

1. Confirm the Galley Ventilation Hood is in the condition required before operation.
2. Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, do not operate and
raise a defect record.
3. Record the check in the crew training record system.
4. Report any a spare part fitted without the traceability record to the Director of Marine
Operations before the vessel next sails.

Records are retained on board and ashore and are subject to review during internal audit and
external inspection.
