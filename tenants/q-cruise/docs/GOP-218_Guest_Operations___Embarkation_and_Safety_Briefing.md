## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Zenith and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the safety
reporting database and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, embarkation is paused
and the Hotel Operations Manager is notified before the next port of call.

Requirement basis: ISM Code Section 7 — development of plans for shipboard operations.
