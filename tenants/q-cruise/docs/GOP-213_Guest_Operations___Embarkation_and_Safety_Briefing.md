## Procedure

This procedure governs guest embarkation aboard MV Q-Horizon and the associated safety
briefing.

All guests must complete the briefing before departure. Attendance is recorded in the
planned maintenance system and reconciled against the manifest.

Where the monitoring instrument is outside its calibration interval, embarkation is paused
and the Fleet Technical Superintendent is notified before the vessel next sails.

Requirement basis: ISM Code Section 7 — development of plans for shipboard operations.
