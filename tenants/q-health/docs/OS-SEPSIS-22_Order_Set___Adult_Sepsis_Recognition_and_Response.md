## Contents and Deployment

Order set supporting the Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response pathway (CP-SEPSIS-01),
deployed in the electronic health record.

This set implements pathway CP-SEPSIS-01 under clinical policy CP-POL-300, supported by
decision rule CDS-029. It is presented to the ordering clinician when a change in level of
consciousness recorded on reassessment. Default selections reflect the pathway; each may be
deselected with a documented reason.

Contents include the initial assessment bundle, the associated laboratory requests, and the
escalation task routed to the responsible clinician before the next scheduled medication
round.

Changes to this order set require approval by the Quality and Patient Safety Director and
are validated in the non-production environment before release.
