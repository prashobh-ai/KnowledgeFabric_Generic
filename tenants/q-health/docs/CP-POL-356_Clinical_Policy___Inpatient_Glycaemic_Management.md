## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-356 governing the conduct of care described in the
Inpatient Glycaemic Management pathway (CP-DIAB-02).

It is the policy of the organisation that a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified
at admission results in a documented clinical response. Staff must escalate to the
responsible clinician within the stated interval within 60 minutes of recognition.

Regulatory basis: Accreditation Standard IC.02.01.01 — infection prevention and control
activities.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Quality and Patient Safety Director owns this policy and is accountable for its
currency. Review occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or
regulation changes.

The Medical Staff Committee Chair approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are
published to the pharmacy verification queue and the superseded version is retained in the
accreditation evidence repository.
