## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Community Acquired Pneumonia pathway (CP-PNEU-01).

Observation: in a sample of records, an unexplained reduction in urine output over the
preceding interval was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy
CP-POL-363 and pathway CP-PNEU-01. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-281.

Standard: 42 CFR 482.13 — patient rights under the Conditions of Participation.

Required action: reinforce that staff must review the medication list against the
reconciliation record before the next scheduled medication round, and re-audit at the next
cycle. Owner: Clinical Governance Lead.
