## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 42 CFR 482.21 — quality assessment and performance
improvement programme.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-349 and the
associated Multimodal Analgesia pathway (CP-PAIN-05).

Sampled records show that refer to the specialist team using the standard referral pathway
was performed at each documented reassessment in the majority of eligible episodes. The
reported measure is the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Quality and Patient
Safety Director.
