## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic
protected health information.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-300 and the
associated Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response pathway (CP-SEPSIS-01).

Sampled records show that review the medication list against the reconciliation record was
performed before the next scheduled medication round in the majority of eligible episodes.
The reported measure is the proportion of eligible patients with the assessment completed on
time.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Care Pathway Owner.
