## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance pathway (CP-AKI-01).

Observation: in a sample of records, a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified at
admission was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy CP-POL-328
and pathway CP-AKI-01. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-284.

Standard: 42 CFR 482.23 — nursing services and staffing requirements.

Required action: reinforce that staff must repeat the assessment and document the outcome in
the record within 60 minutes of recognition, and re-audit at the next cycle. Owner: Pharmacy
and Therapeutics Chair.
