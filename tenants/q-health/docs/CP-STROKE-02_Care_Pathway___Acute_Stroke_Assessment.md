## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Acute Stroke Assessment (CP-STROKE-02) across all
inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-314 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the clinical decision support engine. Bedside execution follows SOP-
CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval. On
recognition, staff must refer to the specialist team using the standard referral pathway
within 24 hours of admission.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Care Pathway Owner. The reported measure is the
proportion of eligible patients with the assessment completed on time.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under Accreditation Standard IC.02.01.01.
