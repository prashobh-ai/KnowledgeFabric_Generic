# Care Pathway — Falls Risk Assessment

## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Falls Risk Assessment (CP-FALLS-03) across all
inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-370 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the computerised provider order entry module. Bedside execution follows SOP-
CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval. On
recognition, staff must escalate to the responsible clinician within the stated interval
before the next scheduled medication round.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Director of Nursing Practice. The reported measure is
the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under Accreditation Standard MM.05.01.01.
