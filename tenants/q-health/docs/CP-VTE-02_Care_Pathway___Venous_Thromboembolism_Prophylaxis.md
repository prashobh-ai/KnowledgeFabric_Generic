# Care Pathway — Venous Thromboembolism Prophylaxis

## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Venous Thromboembolism Prophylaxis (CP-VTE-02)
across all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-335 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the results review workspace. Bedside execution follows SOP-CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval. On
recognition, staff must escalate to the responsible clinician within the stated interval
within one nursing shift.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Quality and Patient Safety Director. The reported
measure is the median interval from recognition to first intervention.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 45 CFR 164.312.
