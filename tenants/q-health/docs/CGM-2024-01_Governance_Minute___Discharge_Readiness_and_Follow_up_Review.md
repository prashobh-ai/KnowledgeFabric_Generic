## Discussion and Decision

Minute of the committee reviewing clinical policy CP-POL-377 and the associated Discharge
Readiness and Follow-up pathway (CP-DISCH-01).

The committee noted the proportion of records with authenticated entries within the required
period and the level of documented variance. Discussion focused on whether a change in level
of consciousness recorded on reassessment is being recognised reliably at the point of care.

Decision: the pathway is reaffirmed without substantive change. The Pharmacy and
Therapeutics Chair will report the measure again at the next meeting.
