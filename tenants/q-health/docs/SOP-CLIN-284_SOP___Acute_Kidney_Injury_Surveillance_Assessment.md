# SOP — Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance Assessment

**Document number:** SOP-CLIN-284  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Priya Delacroix (Director of Nursing Practice, STF-7076)  
**Approved by:** Idris Okonjo (Medical Staff Committee Chair, STF-7148)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-AKI-01.
2. Record the assessment in the computerised provider order entry module using the
structured template.
3. Where an incomplete allergy record at the point of order entry, refer to the specialist
team using the standard referral pathway.
4. Repeat the assessment within 24 hours of admission until the criteria are no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
