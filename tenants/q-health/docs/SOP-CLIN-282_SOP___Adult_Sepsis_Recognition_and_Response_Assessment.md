# SOP — Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response Assessment

**Document number:** SOP-CLIN-282  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Anita Haverford (Quality and Patient Safety Director, STF-9506)  
**Approved by:** Marcus Solberg (Director of Nursing Practice, STF-4672)  
**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-SEPSIS-01.
2. Record the assessment in the accreditation evidence repository using the structured
template.
3. Where an incomplete allergy record at the point of order entry, review the medication
list against the reconciliation record.
4. Repeat the assessment before the next scheduled medication round until the criteria are
no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
