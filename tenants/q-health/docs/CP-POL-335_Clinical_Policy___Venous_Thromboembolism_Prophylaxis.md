## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-335 governing the conduct of care described in the
Venous Thromboembolism Prophylaxis pathway (CP-VTE-02).

It is the policy of the organisation that a documented early warning score above the
escalation threshold results in a documented clinical response. Staff must refer to the
specialist team using the standard referral pathway at each documented reassessment.

Regulatory basis: 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards and workforce access
management.
