## Discussion and Decision

Minute of the committee reviewing clinical policy CP-POL-335 and the associated Venous
Thromboembolism Prophylaxis pathway (CP-VTE-02).

The committee noted the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale and the level of
documented variance. Discussion focused on whether an incomplete allergy record at the point
of order entry is being recognised reliably at the point of care.

Decision: the pathway is reaffirmed without substantive change. The Infection Prevention
Lead will report the measure again at the next meeting.
