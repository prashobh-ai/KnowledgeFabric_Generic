## Discussion and Decision

Minute of the committee reviewing clinical policy CP-POL-300 and the associated Adult Sepsis
Recognition and Response pathway (CP-SEPSIS-01).

The committee noted the proportion of eligible patients with the assessment completed on
time and the level of documented variance. Discussion focused on whether a medication
reconciliation discrepancy identified at admission is being recognised reliably at the point
of care.

Decision: the pathway is reaffirmed without substantive change. The Clinical Governance Lead
will report the measure again at the next meeting.
