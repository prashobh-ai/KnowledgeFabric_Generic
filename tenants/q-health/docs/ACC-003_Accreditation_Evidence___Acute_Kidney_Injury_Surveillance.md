## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic
protected health information.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-328 and the
associated Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance pathway (CP-AKI-01).

Sampled records show that initiate the associated order set through the order entry module
was performed within 24 hours of admission in the majority of eligible episodes. The
reported measure is the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Care Pathway Owner.
