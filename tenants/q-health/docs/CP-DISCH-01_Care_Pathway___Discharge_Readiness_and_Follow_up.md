## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Discharge Readiness and Follow-up (CP-DISCH-01)
across all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-377 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the clinical decision support engine. Bedside execution follows SOP-
CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: a serum lactate result returned above the reference range. On recognition,
staff must initiate the associated order set through the order entry module within 24 hours
of admission.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Clinical Informatics Lead. The reported measure is the
proportion of eligible patients with the assessment completed on time.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 42 CFR 482.23.
