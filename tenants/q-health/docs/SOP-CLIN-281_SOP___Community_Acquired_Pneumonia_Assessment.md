# SOP — Community Acquired Pneumonia Assessment

**Document number:** SOP-CLIN-281

## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-PNEU-01.
2. Record the assessment in the care management registry using the structured template.
3. Where an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval, review the
medication list against the reconciliation record.
4. Repeat the assessment within one nursing shift until the criteria are no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
