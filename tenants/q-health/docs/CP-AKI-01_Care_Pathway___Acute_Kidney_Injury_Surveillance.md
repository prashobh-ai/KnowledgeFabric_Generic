# Care Pathway — Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance

## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance (CP-AKI-01) across
all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-328 and implemented through order set
OS-AKI-24 in the care management registry. Bedside execution follows SOP-CLIN-284.

Entry criterion: a serum lactate result returned above the reference range. On recognition,
staff must review the medication list against the reconciliation record within 60 minutes of
recognition.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Director of Nursing Practice. The reported measure is
the median interval from recognition to first intervention.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 42 CFR 482.21.
