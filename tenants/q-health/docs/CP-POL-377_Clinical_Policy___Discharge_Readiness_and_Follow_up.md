## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-377 governing the conduct of care described in the
Discharge Readiness and Follow-up pathway (CP-DISCH-01).

It is the policy of the organisation that an unexplained reduction in urine output over the
preceding interval results in a documented clinical response. Staff must repeat the
assessment and document the outcome in the record within one nursing shift.

Regulatory basis: Accreditation Standard IC.02.01.01 — infection prevention and control
activities.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Director of Nursing Practice owns this policy and is accountable for its currency.
Review occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation
changes.

The Care Pathway Owner approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are published to
the clinical decision support engine and the superseded version is retained in the
