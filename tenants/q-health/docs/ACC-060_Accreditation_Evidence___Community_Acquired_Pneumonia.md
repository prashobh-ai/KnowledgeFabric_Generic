## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of Accreditation Standard RC.01.03.01 — medical record entry
authentication and timeliness.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-363 and the
associated Community Acquired Pneumonia pathway (CP-PNEU-01).

Sampled records show that initiate the associated order set through the order entry module
was performed before the next scheduled medication round in the majority of eligible
episodes. The reported measure is the median interval from recognition to first
intervention.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Director of Nursing
Practice.
