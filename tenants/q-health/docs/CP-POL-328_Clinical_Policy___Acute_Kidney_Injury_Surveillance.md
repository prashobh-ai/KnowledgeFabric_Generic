## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-328 governing the conduct of care described in the
Acute Kidney Injury Surveillance pathway (CP-AKI-01).

It is the policy of the organisation that a change in level of consciousness recorded on
reassessment results in a documented clinical response. Staff must review the medication
list against the reconciliation record within 24 hours of admission.

Regulatory basis: Accreditation Standard IC.02.01.01 — infection prevention and control
activities.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Chief Medical Information Officer owns this policy and is accountable for its currency.
Review occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation
changes.

The Clinical Informatics Lead approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are
published to the results review workspace and the superseded version is retained in the
accreditation evidence repository.
