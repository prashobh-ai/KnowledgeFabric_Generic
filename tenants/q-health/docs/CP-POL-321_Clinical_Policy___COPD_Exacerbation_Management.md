## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-321 governing the conduct of care described in the
COPD Exacerbation Management pathway (CP-COPD-03).

It is the policy of the organisation that a change in level of consciousness recorded on
reassessment results in a documented clinical response. Staff must escalate to the
responsible clinician within the stated interval within 24 hours of admission.

Regulatory basis: 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic protected health
information.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Chief Medical Information Officer owns this policy and is accountable for its currency.
Review occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation
changes.

The Infection Prevention Lead approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are
published to the care management registry and the superseded version is retained in the
accreditation evidence repository.
