## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-370 governing the conduct of care described in the
Falls Risk Assessment pathway (CP-FALLS-03).

It is the policy of the organisation that a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified
at admission results in a documented clinical response. Staff must document the rationale
where the pathway is not followed within 24 hours of admission.

Regulatory basis: 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic protected health
information.
