## Trigger and Action

Decision support rule supporting clinical policy CP-POL-321 and the COPD Exacerbation
Management pathway (CP-COPD-03).

Enforces clinical policy CP-POL-321 for pathway CP-COPD-03, alongside order set OS-COPD-19.

Trigger: a documented early warning score above the escalation threshold.

Action: present an interruptive advisory to the ordering clinician recommending that they
document the rationale where the pathway is not followed. The advisory records the response,
including override and the reason given.

Override rates are monitored by the Chief Medical Information Officer; a sustained rate
above threshold triggers review of the rule for alert burden.
