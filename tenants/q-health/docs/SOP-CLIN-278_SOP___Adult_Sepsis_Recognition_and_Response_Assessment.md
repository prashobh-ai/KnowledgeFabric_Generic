# SOP — Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response Assessment

**Document number:** SOP-CLIN-278  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Farah Sandoval (Health Information Management Director, STF-3911)  
**Approved by:** Mei Vasquez (Health Information Management Director, STF-6540)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-SEPSIS-01.
2. Record the assessment in the accreditation evidence repository using the structured
template.
3. Where a documented early warning score above the escalation threshold, escalate to the
responsible clinician within the stated interval.
4. Repeat the assessment within 24 hours of admission until the criteria are no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
