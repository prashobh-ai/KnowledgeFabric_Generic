# Clinical Audit Finding — Heart Failure Admission and Transition

**Document number:** CAF-2025-002

## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Heart Failure Admission and Transition pathway (CP-CHF-04).

Observation: in a sample of records, a change in level of consciousness recorded on
reassessment was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy CP-
POL-307 and pathway CP-CHF-04. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-283.

Standard: 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic protected health information.

Required action: reinforce that staff must document the rationale where the pathway is not
followed within one nursing shift, and re-audit at the next cycle. Owner: Health Information
Management Director.
