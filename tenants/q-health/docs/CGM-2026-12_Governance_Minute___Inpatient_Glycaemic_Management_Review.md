## Discussion and Decision

Minute of the committee reviewing clinical policy CP-POL-356 and the associated Inpatient
Glycaemic Management pathway (CP-DIAB-02).

The committee noted the proportion of records with authenticated entries within the required
period and the level of documented variance. Discussion focused on whether an incomplete
allergy record at the point of order entry is being recognised reliably at the point of
care.

Decision: the pathway is reaffirmed without substantive change. The Clinical Governance Lead
will report the measure again at the next meeting.
