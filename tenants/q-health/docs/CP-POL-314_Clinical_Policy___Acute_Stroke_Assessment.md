## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-314 governing the conduct of care described in the
Acute Stroke Assessment pathway (CP-STROKE-02).

It is the policy of the organisation that an unexplained reduction in urine output over the
preceding interval results in a documented clinical response. Staff must review the
medication list against the reconciliation record before the next scheduled medication
round.

Regulatory basis: 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic protected health
information.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Infection Prevention Lead owns this policy and is accountable for its currency. Review
occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation changes.

The Clinical Governance Lead approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are
published to the pharmacy verification queue and the superseded version is retained in the
accreditation evidence repository.
