## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Heart Failure Admission and Transition (CP-CHF-04)
across all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-307 and implemented through order set
OS-CHF-23 in the accreditation evidence repository. Bedside execution follows SOP-CLIN-283.

Entry criterion: an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval. On
recognition, staff must repeat the assessment and document the outcome in the record within
one nursing shift.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Chief Medical Information Officer. The reported measure
is the median interval from recognition to first intervention.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 42 CFR 482.21.
