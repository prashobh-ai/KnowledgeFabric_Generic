## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards and workforce
access management.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-342 and the
associated Delirium Screening and Prevention pathway (CP-DELIR-01).

Sampled records show that initiate the associated order set through the order entry module
was performed before the next scheduled medication round in the majority of eligible
episodes. The reported measure is the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Infection
Prevention Lead.
