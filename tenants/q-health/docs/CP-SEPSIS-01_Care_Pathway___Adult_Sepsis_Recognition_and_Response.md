## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response (CP-SEPSIS-01)
across all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-300 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the pharmacy verification queue. Bedside execution follows SOP-CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: an incomplete allergy record at the point of order entry. On recognition,
staff must repeat the assessment and document the outcome in the record within 60 minutes of
recognition.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Clinical Informatics Lead. The reported measure is the
rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 42 CFR 482.21.
