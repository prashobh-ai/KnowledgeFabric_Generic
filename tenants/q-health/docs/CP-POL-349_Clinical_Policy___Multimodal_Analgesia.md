## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-349 governing the conduct of care described in the
Multimodal Analgesia pathway (CP-PAIN-05).

It is the policy of the organisation that a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified
at admission results in a documented clinical response. Staff must review the medication
list against the reconciliation record within 60 minutes of recognition.

Regulatory basis: 42 CFR 482.24 — medical record services and content requirements.

## Responsibilities and Review

The Clinical Informatics Lead owns this policy and is accountable for its currency. Review
occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation changes.

The Clinical Informatics Lead approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions are
published to the pharmacy verification queue and the superseded version is retained in the
accreditation evidence repository.
