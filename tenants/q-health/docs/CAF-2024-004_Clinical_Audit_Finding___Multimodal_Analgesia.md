# Clinical Audit Finding — Multimodal Analgesia

**Document number:** CAF-2024-004

**Approved by:** Nikhil Warden (Infection Prevention Lead, STF-7589)  
**Revision:** F

## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Multimodal Analgesia pathway (CP-PAIN-05).

Observation: in a sample of records, an unexplained reduction in urine output over the
preceding interval was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy
CP-POL-349 and pathway CP-PAIN-05. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-278.

Standard: Accreditation Standard MM.05.01.01 — medication order review before dispensing.

Required action: reinforce that staff must repeat the assessment and document the outcome in
the record before the next scheduled medication round, and re-audit at the next cycle.
Owner: Director of Nursing Practice.
