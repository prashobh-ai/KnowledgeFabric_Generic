## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards and workforce
access management.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-307 and the
associated Heart Failure Admission and Transition pathway (CP-CHF-04).

Sampled records show that review the medication list against the reconciliation record was
performed within 60 minutes of recognition in the majority of eligible episodes. The
reported measure is the median interval from recognition to first intervention.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Clinical
Informatics Lead.
