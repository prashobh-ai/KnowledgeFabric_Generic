## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-300 governing the conduct of care described in the
Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response pathway (CP-SEPSIS-01).

It is the policy of the organisation that a serum lactate result returned above the
reference range results in a documented clinical response. Staff must escalate to the
responsible clinician within the stated interval before the next scheduled medication round.

Regulatory basis: 42 CFR 482.23 — nursing services and staffing requirements.

## Responsibilities and Review
