# SOP — COPD Exacerbation Management Assessment

**Document number:** SOP-CLIN-279

## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-COPD-03.
2. Record the assessment in the results review workspace using the structured template.
3. Where an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval, document the
rationale where the pathway is not followed.
4. Repeat the assessment within 24 hours of admission until the criteria are no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
