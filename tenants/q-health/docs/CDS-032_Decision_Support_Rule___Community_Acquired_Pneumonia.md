# Decision Support Rule — Community Acquired Pneumonia

## Trigger and Action

Decision support rule supporting clinical policy CP-POL-363 and the Community Acquired
Pneumonia pathway (CP-PNEU-01).

Enforces clinical policy CP-POL-363 for pathway CP-PNEU-01, alongside order set OS-PNEU-21.

Trigger: an unexplained reduction in urine output over the preceding interval.

Action: present an interruptive advisory to the ordering clinician recommending that they
initiate the associated order set through the order entry module. The advisory records the
response, including override and the reason given.

Override rates are monitored by the Clinical Governance Lead; a sustained rate above
threshold triggers review of the rule for alert burden.
