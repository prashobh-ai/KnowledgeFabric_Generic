## Trigger and Action

Decision support rule supporting clinical policy CP-POL-342 and the Delirium Screening and
Prevention pathway (CP-DELIR-01).

Enforces clinical policy CP-POL-342 for pathway CP-DELIR-01, alongside order set OS-
DELIR-20.

Trigger: a serum lactate result returned above the reference range.

Action: present an interruptive advisory to the ordering clinician recommending that they
repeat the assessment and document the outcome in the record. The advisory records the
response, including override and the reason given.

Override rates are monitored by the Medical Staff Committee Chair; a sustained rate above
threshold triggers review of the rule for alert burden.
