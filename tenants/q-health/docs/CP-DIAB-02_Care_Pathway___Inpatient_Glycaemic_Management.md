# Care Pathway — Inpatient Glycaemic Management

## Scope and Entry Criteria

This care pathway describes the assessment, escalation and documentation expected for
patients meeting the entry criteria for Inpatient Glycaemic Management (CP-DIAB-02) across
all inpatient areas of Q-Health.

The pathway is authorised by clinical policy CP-POL-356 and implemented through order set
OS-SEPSIS-18 in the electronic health record. Bedside execution follows SOP-CLIN-278.

Entry criterion: an incomplete allergy record at the point of order entry. On recognition,
staff must document the rationale where the pathway is not followed at each documented
reassessment.

## Variance and Review

Clinical judgement takes precedence over the pathway. Where the pathway is not followed, the
responsible clinician must document the rationale in the record.

Variance is reviewed monthly by the Medical Staff Committee Chair. The reported measure is
the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Sustained variance without documented rationale is escalated through the quality assessment
and performance improvement programme under 45 CFR 164.312.
