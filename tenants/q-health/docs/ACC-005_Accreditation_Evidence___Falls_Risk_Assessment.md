## Evidence of Compliance

Evidence prepared in support of 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards and workforce
access management.

The organisation demonstrates compliance through clinical policy CP-POL-370 and the
associated Falls Risk Assessment pathway (CP-FALLS-03).

Sampled records show that review the medication list against the reconciliation record was
performed before the next scheduled medication round in the majority of eligible episodes.
The reported measure is the rate of pathway variance with documented rationale.

Gaps identified during the internal survey are tracked to closure by the Clinical
Informatics Lead.
