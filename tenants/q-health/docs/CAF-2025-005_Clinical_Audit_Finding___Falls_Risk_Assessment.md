## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Falls Risk Assessment pathway (CP-FALLS-03).

Observation: in a sample of records, a serum lactate result returned above the reference
range was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy CP-POL-370 and
pathway CP-FALLS-03. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-278.

Standard: 42 CFR 482.23 — nursing services and staffing requirements.

Required action: reinforce that staff must initiate the associated order set through the
order entry module before the next scheduled medication round, and re-audit at the next
cycle. Owner: Chief Medical Information Officer.
