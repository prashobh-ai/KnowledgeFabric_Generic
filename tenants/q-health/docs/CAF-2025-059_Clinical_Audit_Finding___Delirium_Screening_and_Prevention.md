# Clinical Audit Finding — Delirium Screening and Prevention

**Document number:** CAF-2025-059

**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Delirium Screening and Prevention pathway (CP-DELIR-01).

Observation: in a sample of records, an unexplained reduction in urine output over the
preceding interval was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy
CP-POL-342 and pathway CP-DELIR-01. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-280.

Standard: 42 CFR 482.21 — quality assessment and performance improvement programme.

Required action: reinforce that staff must repeat the assessment and document the outcome in
the record within one nursing shift, and re-audit at the next cycle. Owner: Quality and
Patient Safety Director.
