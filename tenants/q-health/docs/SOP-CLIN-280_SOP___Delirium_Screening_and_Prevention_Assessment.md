## Procedure

1. Confirm the patient meets the entry criteria for CP-DELIR-01.
2. Record the assessment in the clinical decision support engine using the structured
template.
3. Where a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified at admission, review the
medication list against the reconciliation record.
4. Repeat the assessment within 24 hours of admission until the criteria are no longer met.
5. Document the outcome, including any deviation and its rationale.
