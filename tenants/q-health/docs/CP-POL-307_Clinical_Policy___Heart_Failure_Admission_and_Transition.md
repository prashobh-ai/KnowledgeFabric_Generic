## Policy Statement

Q-Health maintains clinical policy CP-POL-307 governing the conduct of care described in the
Heart Failure Admission and Transition pathway (CP-CHF-04).

It is the policy of the organisation that a medication reconciliation discrepancy identified
at admission results in a documented clinical response. Staff must escalate to the
responsible clinician within the stated interval at each documented reassessment.

Regulatory basis: 45 CFR 164.312 — technical safeguards for electronic protected health
information.

## Responsibilities and Review

Review occurs at least every two years, or sooner where practice, evidence or regulation
changes.

The Quality and Patient Safety Director approves substantive revisions. Approved revisions
