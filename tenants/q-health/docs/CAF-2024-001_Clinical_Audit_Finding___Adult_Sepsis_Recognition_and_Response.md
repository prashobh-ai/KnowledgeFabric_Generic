**Document number:** CAF-2024-001

## Finding and Required Action

Internal clinical audit of the Adult Sepsis Recognition and Response pathway (CP-SEPSIS-01).

Observation: in a sample of records, a documented early warning score above the escalation
threshold was present without a documented response, contrary to clinical policy CP-POL-300
and pathway CP-SEPSIS-01. Procedure reference: SOP-CLIN-278.

Standard: 45 CFR 164.308 — administrative safeguards and workforce access management.

Required action: reinforce that staff must refer to the specialist team using the standard
referral pathway within 24 hours of admission, and re-audit at the next cycle. Owner:
Director of Nursing Practice.
