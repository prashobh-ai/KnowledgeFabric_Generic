## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2410-NEP (Phase II Nephrology Progression)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate the change from baseline in the primary measure in the
population defined by the eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in
accordance with the schedule of assessments.

Conduct follows EU Annex 11 — computerised systems in GMP environments. Site-level execution
is governed by SOP-CL-299.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the clinical trial management system and reconciled against source. Any
a temperature excursion in investigational product storage is recorded as a protocol
deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Regulatory Affairs Director. Deviations meeting the seriousness
criteria are escalated within within 24 hours of awareness.
