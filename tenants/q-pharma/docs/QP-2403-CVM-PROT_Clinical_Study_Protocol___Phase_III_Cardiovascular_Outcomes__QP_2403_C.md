## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2403-CVM (Phase III Cardiovascular Outcomes)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate the pharmacokinetic exposure parameter in the
population defined by the eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in
accordance with the schedule of assessments.

Conduct follows 21 CFR 312.62 — investigator recordkeeping and record retention. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-222.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the electronic trial master file and reconciled against source. Any a
dose administered without the required prior laboratory result is recorded as a protocol
deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Regulatory Affairs Director. Deviations meeting the seriousness
criteria are escalated within within 24 hours of awareness.
