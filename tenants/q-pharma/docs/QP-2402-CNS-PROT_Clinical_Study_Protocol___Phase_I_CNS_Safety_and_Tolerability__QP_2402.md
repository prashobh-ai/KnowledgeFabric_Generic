## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2402-CNS (Phase I CNS Safety and
Tolerability) sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate the pharmacokinetic exposure parameter in the
population defined by the eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in
accordance with the schedule of assessments.

Conduct follows ICH E6(R2) Section 4.9 — investigator records and reports. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-211.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the electronic trial master file and reconciled against source. Any
source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval is recorded as a
protocol deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Medical Monitor. Deviations meeting the seriousness criteria are
escalated within at the next monitoring visit.
