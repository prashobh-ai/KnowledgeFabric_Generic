## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2409-INF (Phase III Infectious Disease
Prevention) sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate time to first event in the population defined by the
eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in accordance with the schedule of
assessments.

Conduct follows ICH E6(R2) Section 4.9 — investigator records and reports. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-288.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the safety database and reconciled against source. Any a dose
administered without the required prior laboratory result is recorded as a protocol
deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Quality Systems Manager. Deviations meeting the seriousness
criteria are escalated within at the next monitoring visit.
