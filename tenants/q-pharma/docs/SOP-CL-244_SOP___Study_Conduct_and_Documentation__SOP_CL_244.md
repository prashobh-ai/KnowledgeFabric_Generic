# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-244)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-244  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Neha Ashgrove (Clinical Trial Manager, STF-3469)  
**Approved by:** Clara Suleiman (Biostatistics Lead, STF-6920)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2405-RES.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under ICH E6(R2) Section 4.9 — investigator records and reports.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the randomisation and supply system at the time it is performed.
3. Where source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval occurs,
raise a deviation record within five working days.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the pharmacokinetic exposure parameter.
5. Route to the Biostatistics Lead for classification and CAPA decision.
