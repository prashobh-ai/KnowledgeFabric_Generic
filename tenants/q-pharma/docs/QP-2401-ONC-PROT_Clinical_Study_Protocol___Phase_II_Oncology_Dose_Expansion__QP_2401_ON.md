## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2401-ONC (Phase II Oncology Dose Expansion)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate the change from baseline in the primary measure in the
population defined by the eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in
accordance with the schedule of assessments.

Conduct follows ICH E3 — structure and content of clinical study reports. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-200.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the document management system and reconciled against source. Any
informed consent documented on a superseded form version is recorded as a protocol deviation
and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Medical Monitor. Deviations meeting the seriousness criteria are
escalated within within 30 days of classification.
