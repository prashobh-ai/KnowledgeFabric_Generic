# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-299)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-299  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Elena Nordstrand (Pharmacovigilance Manager, STF-4540)  
**Approved by:** Idris Castellano (Medical Monitor, STF-6516)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2410-NEP.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under ICH E6(R2) Section 4.9 — investigator records and reports.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the electronic trial master file at the time it is performed.
3. Where an assessment performed outside the protocol visit window occurs, raise a deviation
record within 30 days of classification.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the change from baseline in the primary measure.
5. Route to the Medical Monitor for classification and CAPA decision.
