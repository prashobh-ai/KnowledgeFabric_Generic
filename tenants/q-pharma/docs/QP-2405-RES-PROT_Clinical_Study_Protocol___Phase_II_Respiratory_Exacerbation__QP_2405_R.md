## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2405-RES (Phase II Respiratory Exacerbation)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate the primary efficacy endpoint in the population defined
by the eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in accordance with the
schedule of assessments.

Conduct follows EU Annex 11 — computerised systems in GMP environments. Site-level execution
is governed by SOP-CL-244.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the electronic trial master file and reconciled against source. Any
source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval is recorded as a
protocol deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Clinical Operations Director. Deviations meeting the seriousness
criteria are escalated within within 24 hours of awareness.
