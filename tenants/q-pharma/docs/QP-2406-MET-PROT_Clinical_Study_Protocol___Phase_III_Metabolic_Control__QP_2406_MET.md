## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2406-MET (Phase III Metabolic Control)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate time to first event in the population defined by the
eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in accordance with the schedule of
assessments.

Conduct follows ICH E3 — structure and content of clinical study reports. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-255.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the electronic trial master file and reconciled against source. Any
source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval is recorded as a
protocol deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Data Management Lead. Deviations meeting the seriousness criteria
are escalated within within 30 days of classification.
