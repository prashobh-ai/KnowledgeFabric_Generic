# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-277)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-277  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Hannah Solberg (Biostatistics Lead, STF-1872)  
**Approved by:** Lucia Suleiman (Clinical Trial Manager, STF-7500)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-08-08  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2408-HEM.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under ICH E3 — structure and content of clinical study reports.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the electronic data capture system at the time it is performed.
3. Where a dose administered without the required prior laboratory result occurs, raise a
deviation record within five working days.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the composite safety endpoint.
5. Route to the Clinical Trial Manager for classification and CAPA decision.
