## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2404-IMM (Phase II Immunology Efficacy)
sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate time to first event in the population defined by the
eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in accordance with the schedule of
assessments.

Conduct follows 21 CFR Part 11 — electronic records and electronic signatures. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-233.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the clinical trial management system and reconciled against source. Any
source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval is recorded as a
protocol deviation and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Pharmacovigilance Manager. Deviations meeting the seriousness
criteria are escalated within at the next monitoring visit.
