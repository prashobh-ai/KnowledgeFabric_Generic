# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-200)

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2401-ONC.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under EU Annex 11 — computerised systems in GMP environments.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the clinical trial management system at the time it is performed.
3. Where source data not reconciled to the case report form within the interval occurs,
raise a deviation record within 30 days of classification.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the change from baseline in the primary measure.
5. Route to the Data Management Lead for classification and CAPA decision.
