# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-233)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-233  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Elena Bertrand (Biostatistics Lead, STF-2004)  
**Approved by:** Farah Ashgrove (Data Management Lead, STF-1146)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-10-10  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2404-IMM.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under EU Annex 11 — computerised systems in GMP environments.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the electronic data capture system at the time it is performed.
3. Where a temperature excursion in investigational product storage occurs, raise a
deviation record within 24 hours of awareness.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on time to first event.
5. Route to the Data Management Lead for classification and CAPA decision.
