# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-266)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-266  
**Document type:** Standard Operating Procedure  
**Owner:** Hannah Marchetti (Head of Clinical Quality Assurance, STF-9548)  
**Approved by:** Lucia Marchetti (Data Management Lead, STF-7164)  
**Revision:** C  
**Effective:** 2026-03-03  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2407-DER.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under ICH E3 — structure and content of clinical study reports.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the clinical trial management system at the time it is performed.
3. Where an assessment performed outside the protocol visit window occurs, raise a deviation
record within 24 hours of awareness.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the primary efficacy endpoint.
5. Route to the Data Management Lead for classification and CAPA decision.
