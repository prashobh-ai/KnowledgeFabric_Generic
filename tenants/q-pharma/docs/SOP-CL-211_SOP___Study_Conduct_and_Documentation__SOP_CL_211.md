# SOP — Study Conduct and Documentation (SOP-CL-211)

**Document number:** SOP-CL-211

**Effective:** 2026-06-06  

## Purpose and Scope

This procedure defines how Q-Pharma personnel conduct and document activities for studies
including QP-2402-CNS.

It applies to all staff performing the activity, regardless of location. Compliance is
required under EU Annex 11 — computerised systems in GMP environments.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the current protocol version before performing the activity.
2. Record the activity in the document management system at the time it is performed.
3. Where informed consent documented on a superseded form version occurs, raise a deviation
record within 24 hours of awareness.
4. Assess the deviation for impact on the primary efficacy endpoint.
5. Route to the Data Management Lead for classification and CAPA decision.
