## Objectives and Design

This protocol describes the conduct of study QP-2407-DER (Phase II Dermatology Lesion
Response) sponsored by Q-Pharma.

The primary objective is to evaluate time to first event in the population defined by the
eligibility criteria. Subjects are assessed at each visit in accordance with the schedule of
assessments.

Conduct follows ICH E3 — structure and content of clinical study reports. Site-level
execution is governed by SOP-CL-266.

## Data Handling and Oversight

Data are captured in the clinical trial management system and reconciled against source. Any
informed consent documented on a superseded form version is recorded as a protocol deviation
and assessed for impact on subject safety and data integrity.

Oversight rests with the Head of Clinical Quality Assurance. Deviations meeting the
seriousness criteria are escalated within within five working days.
