## Handling Standard

On notification of a claim under form QF-255, the handler must:

1. Confirm cover was in force and the loss falls within the insuring agreement.
2. Check whether the exclusion concerning loss consequent upon the failure of a system the
insured knew to be defective is engaged.
3. Verify that the insured complied with the condition precedent to notify any material
change in the risk during the period.
4. Reserve in the document library within the required interval.
5. Acknowledge to the insured within five business days of notification.

Coverage declinature requires sign-off by the Reinsurance Manager.
