## Handling Standard

On notification of a claim under form QF-299, the handler must:

1. Confirm cover was in force and the loss falls within the insuring agreement.
2. Check whether the exclusion concerning loss arising from a deliberate act of the insured
or their representative is engaged.
3. Verify that the insured complied with the condition precedent to notify any material
change in the risk during the period.
4. Reserve in the rating engine within the required interval.
5. Acknowledge to the insured before the expiry of the period of insurance.

Coverage declinature requires sign-off by the Claims Technical Lead.
