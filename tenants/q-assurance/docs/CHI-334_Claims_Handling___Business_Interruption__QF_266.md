## Handling Standard

On notification of a claim under form QF-266, the handler must:

1. Confirm cover was in force and the loss falls within the insuring agreement.
2. Check whether the exclusion concerning loss arising from contractual liability assumed
beyond that imposed by law is engaged.
3. Verify that the insured complied with the condition precedent to keep records sufficient
to substantiate a claim.
4. Reserve in the claims management system within the required interval.
5. Acknowledge to the insured within 14 days.

Coverage declinature requires sign-off by the Head of Product Governance.
