## Insuring Agreement

Q-Assurance agrees to indemnify the insured in respect of General Liability loss occurring
during the period of insurance, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of form
QF-211.

Cover operates on the basis stated in the schedule, subject to the limit and the deductible
shown.

## Exclusions and Conditions

This policy does not cover loss arising from circumstances notified under a previous policy.

It is a condition precedent to liability that the insured comply with all statutory
requirements applicable to the operation. Failure to comply may entitle the insurer to
decline or reduce a claim.

Notification: the insured must notify the insurer of any circumstance likely to give rise to
a claim before the expiry of the period of insurance.
