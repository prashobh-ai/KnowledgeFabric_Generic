## Insuring Agreement

Q-Assurance agrees to indemnify the insured in respect of Cyber Liability loss occurring
during the period of insurance, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of form
QF-222.

Cover operates on the basis stated in the schedule, subject to the limit and the deductible

## Exclusions and Conditions

This policy does not cover loss arising from a deliberate act of the insured or their
representative.

It is a condition precedent to liability that the insured notify any material change in the
risk during the period. Failure to comply may entitle the insurer to decline or reduce a
claim.

Notification: the insured must notify the insurer of any circumstance likely to give rise to
a claim as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within 30 days.
