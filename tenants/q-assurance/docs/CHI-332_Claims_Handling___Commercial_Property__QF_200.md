## Handling Standard

On notification of a claim under form QF-200, the handler must:

1. Confirm cover was in force and the loss falls within the insuring agreement.
2. Check whether the exclusion concerning loss consequent upon the failure of a system the
insured knew to be defective is engaged.
3. Verify that the insured complied with the condition precedent to maintain the protections
described in the proposal.
4. Reserve in the reinsurance administration system within the required interval.
5. Acknowledge to the insured as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within 30
days.

Coverage declinature requires sign-off by the Reinsurance Manager.
