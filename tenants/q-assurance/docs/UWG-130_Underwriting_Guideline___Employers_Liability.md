## Appetite and Referral

Appetite for Employers Liability written on form QF-288 is described below.

In appetite where the risk presents no aggravating feature and the insured can evidence that
they comply with all statutory requirements applicable to the operation.

Refer to the Head of Product Governance where the exposure exceeds the delegated limit, or
where the risk involves circumstances that would engage the exclusion concerning loss
consequent upon the failure of a system the insured knew to be defective.

Pricing is derived in the claims management system using the approved rating basis.
