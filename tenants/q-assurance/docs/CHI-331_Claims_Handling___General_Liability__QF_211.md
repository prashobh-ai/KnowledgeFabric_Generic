## Handling Standard

On notification of a claim under form QF-211, the handler must:

1. Confirm cover was in force and the loss falls within the insuring agreement.
2. Check whether the exclusion concerning loss arising from a deliberate act of the insured
or their representative is engaged.
3. Verify that the insured complied with the condition precedent to maintain the protections
described in the proposal.
4. Reserve in the claims management system within the required interval.
5. Acknowledge to the insured before the expiry of the period of insurance.

Coverage declinature requires sign-off by the Portfolio Underwriter.
