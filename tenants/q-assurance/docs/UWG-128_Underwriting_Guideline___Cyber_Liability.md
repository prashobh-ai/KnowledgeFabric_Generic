## Appetite and Referral

Appetite for Cyber Liability written on form QF-222 is described below.

In appetite where the risk presents no aggravating feature and the insured can evidence that
they keep records sufficient to substantiate a claim.

Refer to the Reinsurance Manager where the exposure exceeds the delegated limit, or where
the risk involves circumstances that would engage the exclusion concerning loss arising from
an unrepaired defect identified at a prior survey.

Pricing is derived in the policy administration system using the approved rating basis.
