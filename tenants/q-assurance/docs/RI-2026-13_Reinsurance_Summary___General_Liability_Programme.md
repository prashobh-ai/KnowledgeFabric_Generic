## Structure and Application

The General Liability account written on form QF-211 is protected by an excess of loss
programme.

The programme attaches above the retention and responds per event, subject to the
reinstatement provisions.

Claims exceeding the attachment must be notified to reinsurers as soon as reasonably
practicable and in any event within 30 days. Notification is recorded in the underwriting
workbench.
