## Insuring Agreement

Q-Assurance agrees to indemnify the insured in respect of Professional Indemnity loss
occurring during the period of insurance, subject to the terms, conditions and exclusions of
form QF-233.

Cover operates on the basis stated in the schedule, subject to the limit and the deductible
shown.

## Exclusions and Conditions

This policy does not cover loss arising from a deliberate act of the insured or their
representative.

It is a condition precedent to liability that the insured keep records sufficient to
substantiate a claim. Failure to comply may entitle the insurer to decline or reduce a
claim.

Notification: the insured must notify the insurer of any circumstance likely to give rise to
a claim as soon as reasonably practicable and in any event within 30 days.
