## Appetite and Referral

Appetite for Engineering All Risks written on form QF-299 is described below.

In appetite where the risk presents no aggravating feature and the insured can evidence that
they notify any material change in the risk during the period.

Refer to the Coverage Counsel where the exposure exceeds the delegated limit, or where the
risk involves circumstances that would engage the exclusion concerning loss arising from
wear, tear or gradual deterioration.

Pricing is derived in the policy administration system using the approved rating basis.
