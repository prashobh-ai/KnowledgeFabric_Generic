## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of an unapproved repair scheme found during
records review affecting the Pneumatic system (ATA Chapter 36) on aircraft of the type
operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded
system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-738M and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: carry out a repetitive inspection at the stated interval. The compliance
time is 500 flight hours or 300 cycles, whichever occurs first from the effective date of
this directive.

Under 14 CFR 43.9, content, form and disposition of maintenance records. Operators must
record accomplishment in the airworthiness compliance register before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Line Maintenance
Supervisor.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-738M in the airworthiness compliance
register, cross-referenced to task card TC-28-0362 raised to perform the work. Where a
service bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-24-173.
