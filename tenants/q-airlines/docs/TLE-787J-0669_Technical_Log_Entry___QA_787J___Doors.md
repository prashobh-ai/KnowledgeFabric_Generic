## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-787J: chafing of the harness against a
structural former associated with the Doors system (ATA Chapter 52).

Initial assessment by Certifying Staff (B2) confirmed the reported condition. The defect was
deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2024-0462 was raised to
raise an Engineering Order and defer under the applicable relief.

Deferral applied under MEL-52-166. The entry remains open in the reliability reporting
database and is reviewed at each transit check.
