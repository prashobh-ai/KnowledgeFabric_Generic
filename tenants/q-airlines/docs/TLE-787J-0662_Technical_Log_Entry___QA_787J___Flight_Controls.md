## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-787J: delamination detected during tap
inspection associated with the Flight Controls system (ATA Chapter 27).

Initial assessment by Certifying Staff (B1) confirmed the reported condition. The defect was
deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2024-0462 was raised to
quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design organisation.

Deferral applied under MEL-52-166. The entry remains open in the safety reporting system and
is reviewed at each transit check.
