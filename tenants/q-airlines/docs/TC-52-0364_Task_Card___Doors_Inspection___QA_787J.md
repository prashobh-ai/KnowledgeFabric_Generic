## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to quarantine the part and raise a
technical query with the design organisation on the Doors system (ATA Chapter 52) of
aircraft QA-787J.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2026-0267 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2024-0462. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the safety reporting system.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the doors installation for chafing of the harness against a structural former.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design organisation.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under
EASA Part-M M.A.301, continuing airworthiness tasks.

Only Certifying Staff (B1) holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify this
task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the date
of completion, recorded in the safety reporting system.
