## Relief and Conditions

This entry permits dispatch of aircraft QA-737M with the Navigation item inoperative,
subject to the conditions stated below.

Rectification interval: Category C — 10 consecutive calendar days, excluding the day the
defect was recorded.

Dispatch is permitted provided no further defect is recorded against the same system.
Rectification is tracked through EO-2024-0465. If the condition ceases to be met, the relief
is void and the aircraft must not be dispatched until the defect is rectified.

## Maintenance and Operations Procedures

Maintenance procedure: isolate the affected navigation circuit, secure the control in the
safe position, and placard the affected control in the flight deck.

Operations procedure: the commander must be advised of the inoperative item before
departure. Performance penalties, where applicable, are applied through the dispatch
calculation.

The deferred item must be visible in the electronic technical log and reviewed at each
transit until closed.
