## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of chafing of the harness against a
structural former affecting the Wings system (ATA Chapter 57) on aircraft of the type
operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded
system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-E90R and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: raise an Engineering Order and defer under the applicable relief. The
compliance time is 500 flight hours or 300 cycles, whichever occurs first from the effective
date of this directive.

Under 14 CFR 145.219, repair station recordkeeping. Operators must record accomplishment in
the parts and inventory system before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Quality Manager.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-E90R in the parts and inventory
system, cross-referenced to task card TC-28-0362 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-28-174.
