# Service Bulletin — Air Conditioning Modification

**Document number:** SB-21-172  
**Document type:** Service Bulletin  
**Owner:** Omar Ferrers (Continuing Airworthiness Manager, STF-9882)  
**Approved by:** Amara Bertrand (Head of Airworthiness Review, STF-9933)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Planning Information

This service bulletin introduces a modification to the Air Conditioning system (ATA Chapter
21) to address corrosion beyond allowable limits at the fastener row.

Compliance is recommended at 750 flight hours. Where an airworthiness directive mandates
this bulletin, the directive compliance time takes precedence.

Embodiment on aircraft QA-320N is planned during the next scheduled input and is managed
through engineering order EO-2024-0462. Where mandated, see ADCR-2025-0263.
