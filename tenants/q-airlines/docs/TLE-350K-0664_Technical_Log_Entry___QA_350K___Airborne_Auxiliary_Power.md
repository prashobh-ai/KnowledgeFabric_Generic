## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-350K: chafing of the harness against a
structural former associated with the Airborne Auxiliary Power system (ATA Chapter 49).

Initial assessment by Continuing Airworthiness Manager confirmed the reported condition. The
defect was deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2024-0462 was
raised to raise an Engineering Order and defer under the applicable relief.

Deferral applied under MEL-25-168. The entry remains open in the flight operations
documentation portal and is reviewed at each transit check.
