## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-738M: an out-of-tolerance actuator
response time associated with the Pneumatic system (ATA Chapter 36).

Initial assessment by Base Maintenance Planner confirmed the reported condition. The defect
was deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2025-0463 was raised to
quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design organisation.

Deferral applied under MEL-24-163. The entry remains open in the reliability reporting
database and is reviewed at each transit check.
