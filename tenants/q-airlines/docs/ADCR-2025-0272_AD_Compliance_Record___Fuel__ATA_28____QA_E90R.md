## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of delamination detected during tap
inspection affecting the Fuel system (ATA Chapter 28) on aircraft of the type operated by
Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded system function
during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-E90R and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: replace the affected component with a serviceable unit. The compliance time
is each transit check until terminating action is embodied from the effective date of this
directive.

Under 14 CFR 39.7, operating a product that does not comply with an applicable AD. Operators
must record accomplishment in the planning and forecasting module before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Reliability Engineer.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-E90R in the planning and forecasting
module, cross-referenced to task card TC-28-0362 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-28-174.
