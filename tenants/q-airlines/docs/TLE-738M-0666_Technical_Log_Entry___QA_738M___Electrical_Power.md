## Defect and Rectification

at the fastener row associated with the Electrical Power system (ATA Chapter 24).

deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2025-0463 was raised to
replace the affected component with a serviceable unit.

Deferral applied under MEL-24-163. The entry remains open in the electronic technical log
and is reviewed at each transit check.
