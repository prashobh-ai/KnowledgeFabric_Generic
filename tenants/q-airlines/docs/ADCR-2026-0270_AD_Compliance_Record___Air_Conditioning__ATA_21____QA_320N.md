## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of chafing of the harness against a
structural former affecting the Air Conditioning system (ATA Chapter 21) on aircraft of the
type operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-320N and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: apply the approved repair scheme and record the deviation. The compliance
time is each transit check until terminating action is embodied from the effective date of
this directive.

Under EASA Part-145 145.A.50, certification of maintenance and release to service. Operators
must record accomplishment in the airworthiness compliance register before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Quality Manager.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-320N in the airworthiness compliance
register, cross-referenced to task card TC-28-0362 raised to perform the work. Where a
service bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
