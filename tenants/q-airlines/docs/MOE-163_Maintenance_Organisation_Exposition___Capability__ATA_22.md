## Scope of Approval

Q-Airlines maintains an approved maintenance organisation exposition describing the scope of
work, facilities, personnel and procedures under which maintenance is performed.

The organisation holds capability for the Auto Flight system (ATA Chapter 22) at base and
line level. Work outside the stated capability requires a subcontract arrangement approved
by the Technical Records Controller.

Under 14 CFR 91.403, the owner or operator is primarily responsible for airworthiness.
