## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of chafing of the harness against a
structural former affecting the Flight Controls system (ATA Chapter 27) on aircraft of the
type operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-787J and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design
organisation. The compliance time is 12 calendar months from the effective date of this
directive.

Under EASA Part-CAMO CAMO.A.315, continuing airworthiness management. Operators must record
accomplishment in the planning and forecasting module before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Head of Airworthiness
Review.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-787J in the planning and forecasting
module, cross-referenced to task card TC-52-0364 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
