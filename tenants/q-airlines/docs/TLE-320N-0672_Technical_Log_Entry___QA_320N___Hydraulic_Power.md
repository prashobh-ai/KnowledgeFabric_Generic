## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-320N: a repeat defect recorded on three
consecutive sectors associated with the Hydraulic Power system (ATA Chapter 29).

Initial assessment by Technical Records Controller confirmed the reported condition. The
defect was deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2024-0462 was
raised to quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design organisation.

Deferral applied under MEL-21-162. The entry remains open in the maintenance information
system and is reviewed at each transit check.
