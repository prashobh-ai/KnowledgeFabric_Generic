# AD Compliance Record — Equipment and Furnishings (ATA 25) — QA-350K

## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of an unapproved repair scheme found during
records review affecting the Equipment and Furnishings system (ATA Chapter 25) on aircraft
of the type operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-350K and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: replace the affected component with a serviceable unit. The compliance time
is 12 calendar months from the effective date of this directive.

Under 14 CFR 121.367, maintenance, preventive maintenance and alterations programme.
Operators must record accomplishment in the maintenance information system before further
flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Line Maintenance
Supervisor.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-350K in the maintenance information
system, cross-referenced to task card TC-25-0366 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
