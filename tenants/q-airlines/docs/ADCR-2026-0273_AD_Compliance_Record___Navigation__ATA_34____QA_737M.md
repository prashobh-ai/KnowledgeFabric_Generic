## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of corrosion beyond allowable limits at the
fastener row affecting the Navigation system (ATA Chapter 34) on aircraft of the type
operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded
system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-737M and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: apply the approved repair scheme and record the deviation. The compliance
time is 1,000 flight cycles from the effective date of this directive.

Under 14 CFR 39.7, operating a product that does not comply with an applicable AD. Operators
must record accomplishment in the safety reporting system before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Base Maintenance
Planner.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-737M in the safety reporting system,
cross-referenced to task card TC-34-0363 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
