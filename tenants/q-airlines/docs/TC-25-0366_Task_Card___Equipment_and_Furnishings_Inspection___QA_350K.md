# Task Card — Equipment and Furnishings Inspection — QA-350K

**Document number:** TC-25-0366  
**Document type:** Maintenance Task Card

**Approved by:** Marcus Achterberg (Technical Records Controller, STF-2497)  
**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to replace the affected component with a
serviceable unit on the Equipment and Furnishings system (ATA Chapter 25) of aircraft
QA-350K.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2024-0262 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2024-0462. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the flight operations documentation
portal.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the equipment and furnishings installation for an unapproved repair scheme found
during records review.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Replace the affected component with a serviceable unit.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under
EASA Part-CAMO CAMO.A.315, continuing airworthiness management.

Only Reliability Engineer holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify this
task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the date
of completion, recorded in the flight operations documentation portal.
