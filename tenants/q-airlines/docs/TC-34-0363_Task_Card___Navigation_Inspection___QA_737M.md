# Task Card — Navigation Inspection — QA-737M

**Document number:** TC-34-0363

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to accomplish the inspection at or before
the next A-check on the Navigation system (ATA Chapter 34) of aircraft QA-737M.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2025-0266 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2024-0465. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the reliability reporting database.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the navigation installation for a repeat defect recorded on three consecutive
sectors.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Accomplish the inspection at or before the next a-check.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under 14
CFR 39.15, an AD continues to apply after a product is modified or repaired.

Only Certifying Staff (B1) holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify this
task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the date
of completion, recorded in the reliability reporting database.
