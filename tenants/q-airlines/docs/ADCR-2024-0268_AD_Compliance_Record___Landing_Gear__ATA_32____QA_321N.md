# AD Compliance Record — Landing Gear (ATA 32) — QA-321N

**Document number:** ADCR-2024-0268  
**Document type:** Airworthiness Directive Compliance Record

**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of a repeat defect recorded on three
consecutive sectors affecting the Landing Gear system (ATA Chapter 32) on aircraft of the
type operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-321N and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: carry out a repetitive inspection at the stated interval. The compliance
time is each transit check until terminating action is embodied from the effective date of
this directive.

Under 14 CFR 145.219, repair station recordkeeping. Operators must record accomplishment in
the flight operations documentation portal before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Quality Manager.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-321N in the flight operations
documentation portal, cross-referenced to task card TC-22-0365 raised to perform the work.
Where a service bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
