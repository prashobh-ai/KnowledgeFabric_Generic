# MEL Entry — Electrical Power — ATA 24

**Document number:** MEL-24-163  
**Document type:** Minimum Equipment List Entry  
**Owner:** Marcus Ashgrove (Continuing Airworthiness Manager, STF-8956)  
**Approved by:** Anders Calloway (Certifying Staff (B1), STF-4529)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Relief and Conditions

This entry permits dispatch of aircraft QA-738M with the Electrical Power item inoperative,
subject to the conditions stated below.

Rectification interval: Category C — 10 consecutive calendar days, excluding the day the
defect was recorded.

Dispatch is permitted provided the deferred item remains within the permitted rectification
interval. Rectification is tracked through EO-2025-0463. If the condition ceases to be met,
the relief is void and the aircraft must not be dispatched until the defect is rectified.

## Maintenance and Operations Procedures

Maintenance procedure: isolate the affected electrical power circuit, secure the control in
the safe position, and placard the affected control in the flight deck.

Operations procedure: the commander must be advised of the inoperative item before
departure. Performance penalties, where applicable, are applied through the dispatch
calculation.

The deferred item must be visible in the reliability reporting database and reviewed at each
transit until closed.
