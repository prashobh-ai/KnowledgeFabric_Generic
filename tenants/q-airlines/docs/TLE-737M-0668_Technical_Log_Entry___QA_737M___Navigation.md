## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-737M: chafing of the harness against a
structural former associated with the Navigation system (ATA Chapter 34).

Initial assessment by Technical Records Controller confirmed the reported condition. The
defect was deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2024-0465 was
raised to replace the affected component with a serviceable unit.

Deferral applied under MEL-34-165. The entry remains open in the flight operations
documentation portal and is reviewed at each transit check.
