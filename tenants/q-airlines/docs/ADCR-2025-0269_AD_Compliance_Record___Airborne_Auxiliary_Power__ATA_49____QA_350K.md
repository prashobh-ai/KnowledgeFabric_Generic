# AD Compliance Record — Airborne Auxiliary Power (ATA 49) — QA-350K

**Effective:** 2025-08-08  

## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of moisture ingress at the connector
backshell affecting the Airborne Auxiliary Power system (ATA Chapter 49) on aircraft of the
type operated by Q-Airlines. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-350K and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design
organisation. The compliance time is 750 flight hours from the effective date of this
directive.

Under EASA Part-M M.A.301, continuing airworthiness tasks. Operators must record
accomplishment in the electronic technical log before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Certifying Staff (B1).

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-350K in the electronic technical
log, cross-referenced to task card TC-25-0366 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-172.
