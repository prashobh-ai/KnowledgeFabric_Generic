# Store Instruction — Chilled Ready Meals Handling

**Document number:** SOI-335  
**Document type:** Store Operating Instruction  
**Owner:** Marcus Thornbury (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-6139)  
**Approved by:** Mei Marchetti (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-9827)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Instruction

1. On delivery, check that the article identifier matches the despatch record.
2. Confirm the article meets the requirement that it be maintained within the stated
temperature range at all times.
3. Where a batch identifier that could not be reconciled to the despatch record, quarantine
the stock and record it in the vendor portal.
4. Escalate to the VP Supply Chain Compliance at the next scheduled review.
5. Do not return quarantined stock to sale without authorisation.
