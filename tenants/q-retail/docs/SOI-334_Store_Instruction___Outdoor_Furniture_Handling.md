## Instruction

1. On delivery, check that the article identifier matches the despatch record.
2. Confirm the article meets the requirement that it be free from the restricted substances
listed in the schedule.
3. Where a batch identifier that could not be reconciled to the despatch record, quarantine
the stock and record it in the store stock system.
4. Escalate to the Technical Manager before the next despatch.
5. Do not return quarantined stock to sale without authorisation.
