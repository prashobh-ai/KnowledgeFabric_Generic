# Vendor Requirement — Outdoor Furniture

**Document number:** VCR-123  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Sofia Bertrand (Store Operations Manager, STF-6610)  
**Approved by:** Pieter Ravensworth (Product Safety Lead, STF-9114)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Outdoor Furniture to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2959, must comply with
the requirements in this document.

Every article must be maintained within the stated temperature range at all times. Evidence
must be available for inspection before the next despatch.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the product information management system. Where a batch
identifier that could not be reconciled to the despatch record, the despatch is held pending
correction.
