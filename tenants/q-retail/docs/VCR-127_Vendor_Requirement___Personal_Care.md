# Vendor Requirement — Personal Care

**Document number:** VCR-127  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Lucia Bertrand (Category Buyer, STF-4903)  
**Approved by:** Nikhil Warden (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-4661)  
**Revision:** F  
**Effective:** 2026-06-06  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Personal Care to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2685, must comply with the
requirements in this document.

Every article must be labelled with the country of origin and the batch identifier. Evidence
must be available for inspection at the next scheduled review.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the store stock system. Where a temperature excursion recorded
during transit, the despatch is held pending correction.
