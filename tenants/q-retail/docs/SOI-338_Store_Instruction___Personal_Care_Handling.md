# Store Instruction — Personal Care Handling

**Document number:** SOI-338  
**Document type:** Store Operating Instruction  
**Owner:** Anders Calloway (Product Safety Lead, STF-9500)  
**Approved by:** Omar Okonjo (Technical Manager, STF-6794)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Instruction

1. On delivery, check that the article identifier matches the despatch record.
2. Confirm the article meets the requirement that it be labelled with the country of origin
and the batch identifier.
3. Where an unapproved change to the component specification, quarantine the stock and
record it in the store stock system.
4. Escalate to the Product Safety Lead at the next scheduled review.
5. Do not return quarantined stock to sale without authorisation.
