# Vendor Requirement — Chilled Ready Meals

**Document number:** VCR-124  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Sofia Achterberg (Category Buyer, STF-6126)  
**Approved by:** Omar Warden (Distribution Centre Manager, STF-3642)  
**Revision:** C  
**Effective:** 2026-03-03  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Chilled Ready Meals to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2000, must comply
with the requirements in this document.

Every article must be free from the restricted substances listed in the schedule. Evidence
must be available for inspection within five working days.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the store stock system. Where a labelling discrepancy identified
at goods receipt, the despatch is held pending correction.
