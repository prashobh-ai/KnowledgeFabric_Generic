# Recall Notice — Fresh Produce (ART-82924)

**Document number:** RCL-2026-024  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Clara Castellano (Supplier Quality Auditor, STF-7183)  
**Approved by:** Sofia Achterberg (Supplier Quality Auditor, STF-1995)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-82924 in the Fresh Produce range, supplied by vendor
VEN-2548.

Reason: a labelling discrepancy identified at goods receipt, meaning article ART-82924 does
not carry the age grading and associated safety warnings. Specification: ART-82924; vendor
requirement: VCR-122.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the product
information management system, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under Restricted substances schedule — limits on substances of
concern in consumer articles.
