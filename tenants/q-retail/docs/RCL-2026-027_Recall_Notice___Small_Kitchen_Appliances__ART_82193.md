# Recall Notice — Small Kitchen Appliances (ART-82193)

**Document number:** RCL-2026-027  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Omar Trelawney (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-2644)  
**Approved by:** Omar Achterberg (Technical Manager, STF-6116)  
**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-82193 in the Small Kitchen Appliances range, supplied by
vendor VEN-2411.

Reason: a batch identifier that could not be reconciled to the despatch record, meaning
article ART-82193 does not carry the correct allergen declaration on the outer pack.
Specification: ART-82193; vendor requirement: VCR-125.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the
traceability event repository, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under General Product Safety requirements — obligation to place
only safe products on the market.
