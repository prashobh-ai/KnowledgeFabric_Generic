# Vendor Requirement — Small Kitchen Appliances

**Document number:** VCR-125  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Amara Eriksen (Store Operations Manager, STF-3920)  
**Approved by:** Farah Marchetti (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-3889)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Small Kitchen Appliances to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2411, must
comply with the requirements in this document.

Every article must be maintained within the stated temperature range at all times. Evidence
must be available for inspection within 48 hours.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the recall coordination tool. Where a test certificate that had
expired at the point of despatch, the despatch is held pending correction.
