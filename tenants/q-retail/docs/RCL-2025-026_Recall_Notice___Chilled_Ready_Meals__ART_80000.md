# Recall Notice — Chilled Ready Meals (ART-80000)

**Document number:** RCL-2025-026  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Yusuf Calloway (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-1931)  
**Approved by:** Pieter Sandoval (Product Safety Lead, STF-5435)  
**Revision:** D  
**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-80000 in the Chilled Ready Meals range, supplied by vendor
VEN-2000.

Reason: a test certificate that had expired at the point of despatch, meaning article
ART-80000 does not be labelled with the country of origin and the batch identifier.
Specification: ART-80000; vendor requirement: VCR-124.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the vendor
portal, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under Food traceability requirements — one-step-back and one-
step-forward traceability records.
