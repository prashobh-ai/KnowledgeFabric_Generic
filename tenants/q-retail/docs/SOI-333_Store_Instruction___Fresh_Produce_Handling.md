# Store Instruction — Fresh Produce Handling

**Document number:** SOI-333  
**Document type:** Store Operating Instruction  
**Owner:** Lucia Solberg (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-6335)  
**Approved by:** Marcus Ashgrove (Store Operations Manager, STF-2694)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Instruction

1. On delivery, check that the article identifier matches the despatch record.
2. Confirm the article meets the requirement that it carry the age grading and associated
safety warnings.
3. Where a batch identifier that could not be reconciled to the despatch record, quarantine
the stock and record it in the quality management platform.
4. Escalate to the VP Supply Chain Compliance within five working days.
5. Do not return quarantined stock to sale without authorisation.
