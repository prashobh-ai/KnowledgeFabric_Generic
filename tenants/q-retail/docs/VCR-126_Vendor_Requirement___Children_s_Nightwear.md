# Vendor Requirement — Children's Nightwear

**Document number:** VCR-126  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Jonas Vasquez (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-2045)  
**Approved by:** Idris Warden (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-7414)  
**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Children's Nightwear to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2274, must comply
with the requirements in this document.

Every article must be free from the restricted substances listed in the schedule. Evidence
must be available for inspection within 48 hours.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the quality management platform. Where a test certificate that had
expired at the point of despatch, the despatch is held pending correction.
