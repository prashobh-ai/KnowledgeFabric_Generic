# Store Instruction — Small Kitchen Appliances Handling

**Document number:** SOI-336

**Revision:** E  
**Effective:** 2025-05-05  

## Instruction

1. On delivery, check that the article identifier matches the despatch record.
2. Confirm the article meets the requirement that it be maintained within the stated
temperature range at all times.
3. Where an unapproved change to the component specification, quarantine the stock and
record it in the vendor portal.
4. Escalate to the Distribution Centre Manager before the next despatch.
5. Do not return quarantined stock to sale without authorisation.
