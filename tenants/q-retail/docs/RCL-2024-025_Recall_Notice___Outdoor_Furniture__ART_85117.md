# Recall Notice — Outdoor Furniture (ART-85117)

**Document number:** RCL-2024-025  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Marcus Solberg (Category Buyer, STF-8801)  
**Approved by:** Farah Suleiman (Distribution Centre Manager, STF-4945)  
**Revision:** C  
**Effective:** 2026-03-03  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-85117 in the Outdoor Furniture range, supplied by vendor
VEN-2959.

Reason: a temperature excursion recorded during transit, meaning article ART-85117 does not
carry the correct allergen declaration on the outer pack. Specification: ART-85117; vendor
requirement: VCR-123.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the store
stock system, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under Food traceability requirements — one-step-back and one-
step-forward traceability records.
