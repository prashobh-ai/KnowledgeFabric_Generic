# Vendor Requirement — Fresh Produce

**Document number:** VCR-122  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Sofia Ravensworth (Store Operations Manager, STF-6924)  
**Approved by:** Idris Holbrook (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-3675)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Fresh Produce to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-2548, must comply with the
requirements in this document.

Every article must meet the applicable flammability performance standard. Evidence must be
available for inspection before the next despatch.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the store stock system. Where a temperature excursion recorded
during transit, the despatch is held pending correction.
