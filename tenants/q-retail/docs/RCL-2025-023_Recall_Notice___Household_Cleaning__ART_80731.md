# Recall Notice — Household Cleaning (ART-80731)

**Document number:** RCL-2025-023  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Priya Ashgrove (Supplier Quality Auditor, STF-9311)  
**Approved by:** Ravi Rahimi (Technical Manager, STF-6572)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-80731 in the Household Cleaning range, supplied by vendor
VEN-2137.

Reason: a test certificate that had expired at the point of despatch, meaning article
ART-80731 does not carry the age grading and associated safety warnings. Specification:
ART-80731; vendor requirement: VCR-129.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the product
information management system, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under Restricted substances schedule — limits on substances of
concern in consumer articles.
