# Recall Notice — Children's Nightwear (ART-81462)

**Document number:** RCL-2024-028  
**Document type:** Product Recall Notice  
**Owner:** Farah Calloway (Product Safety Lead, STF-7763)  
**Approved by:** Anders Thornbury (Category Buyer, STF-9828)  
**Revision:** F  
**Effective:** 2026-06-06  

## Recall Detail and Action

Q-Retail is recalling article ART-81462 in the Children's Nightwear range, supplied by
vendor VEN-2274.

Reason: a test certificate that had expired at the point of despatch, meaning article
ART-81462 does not be maintained within the stated temperature range at all times.
Specification: ART-81462; vendor requirement: VCR-126.

Stores must remove affected stock from sale immediately, record the quantity in the product
information management system, and display the customer notice at the point of sale.

Regulatory notification made under Textile labelling requirements — fibre composition and
care labelling.
