# Vendor Requirement — Bakery

**Document number:** VCR-128  
**Document type:** Vendor Compliance Requirement  
**Owner:** Clara Suleiman (VP Supply Chain Compliance, STF-2044)  
**Approved by:** Yusuf Warden (Category Buyer, STF-8121)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Requirement

Vendors supplying Bakery to Q-Retail, including vendor VEN-3096, must comply with the
requirements in this document.

Every article must carry the correct allergen declaration on the outer pack. Evidence must
be available for inspection at the next scheduled review.

Non-compliance may result in the affected article being blocked at goods receipt and a
chargeback applied.

## Traceability

Each despatch must carry the article identifier and the location identifier so that a unit
can be traced from source to shelf.

Data is exchanged through the vendor portal. Where a labelling discrepancy identified at
goods receipt, the despatch is held pending correction.
