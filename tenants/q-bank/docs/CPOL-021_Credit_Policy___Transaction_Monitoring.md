## Policy Requirement

It is the policy of Q-Bank that material outsourcing arrangements are subject to due
diligence and ongoing monitoring before an exposure is approved.

Exceptions require approval at the delegated authority level and must be recorded with
rationale in the model inventory.

This policy discharges obligation OBL-113 and is evidenced through control CTL-417.
