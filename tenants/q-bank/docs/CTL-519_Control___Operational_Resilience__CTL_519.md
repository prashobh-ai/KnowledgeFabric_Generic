## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-519 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-191 concerning Operational Resilience.

Implements policy CPOL-015 and is executed through PROC-229.

Objective: ensure that exposures are rated using the approved methodology and reviewed at
least annually.

Operation: the control executes in the governance risk and compliance platform. Exceptions
are routed to the Head of Internal Audit for disposition at the next scheduled committee.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the median days
from alert to disposition.
