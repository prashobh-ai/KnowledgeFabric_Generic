## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-400 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-100 concerning Customer Due Diligence.

Implements policy CPOL-016 and is executed through PROC-230.

Objective: ensure that collateral is valued using an independent source at the required
frequency.

Operation: the control executes in the credit origination workflow. Exceptions are routed to
the Financial Crime Lead for disposition by the end of the following month.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the exception rate
against the approved standard.
