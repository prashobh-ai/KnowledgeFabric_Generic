## Policy Requirement

It is the policy of Q-Bank that exposures are rated using the approved methodology and
reviewed at least annually before an exposure is approved.

Exceptions require approval at the delegated authority level and must be recorded with
rationale in the customer onboarding portal.

This policy discharges obligation OBL-204 and is evidenced through control CTL-536.
