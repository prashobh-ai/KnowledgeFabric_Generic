## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-468 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-152 concerning Complaints Handling.

Implements policy CPOL-014 and is executed through PROC-228.

Objective: ensure that material outsourcing arrangements are subject to due diligence and
ongoing monitoring.

Operation: the control executes in the credit origination workflow. Exceptions are routed to
the Model Risk Manager for disposition within five business days.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the proportion of
reviews completed within the required period.
