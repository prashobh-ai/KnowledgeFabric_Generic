# Control — Transaction Monitoring (CTL-417)

**Document number:** CTL-417

## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-417 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-113 concerning Transaction Monitoring.

Implements policy CPOL-021 and is executed through PROC-227.

Objective: ensure that material outsourcing arrangements are subject to due diligence and
ongoing monitoring.

Operation: the control executes in the transaction monitoring system. Exceptions are routed
to the Head of Internal Audit for disposition within 24 hours of identification.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the proportion of
executions with complete evidence retained.
