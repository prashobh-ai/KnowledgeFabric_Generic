## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-434 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-126 concerning Credit Risk Rating.

Implements policy CPOL-018 and is executed through PROC-226.

Objective: ensure that material outsourcing arrangements are subject to due diligence and
ongoing monitoring.

Operation: the control executes in the governance risk and compliance platform. Exceptions
are routed to the Chief Risk Officer for disposition by the end of the following month.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the median days
from alert to disposition.
