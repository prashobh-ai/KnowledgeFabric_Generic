## Policy Requirement

It is the policy of Q-Bank that identity is verified and the source of funds is understood
before onboarding before an exposure is approved.

Exceptions require approval at the delegated authority level and must be recorded with
rationale in the transaction monitoring system.

This policy discharges obligation OBL-165 and is evidenced through control CTL-485.
