## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-502 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-178 concerning Outsourcing Oversight.

Implements policy CPOL-014 and is executed through PROC-224.

Objective: ensure that alerts are dispositioned by a competent reviewer with documented
rationale.

Operation: the control executes in the customer onboarding portal. Exceptions are routed to
the Control Owner for disposition by the end of the following month.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the proportion of
reviews completed within the required period.
