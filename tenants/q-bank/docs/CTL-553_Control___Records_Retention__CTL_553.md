## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-553 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-217 concerning Records Retention.

Implements policy CPOL-014 and is executed through PROC-225.

Objective: ensure that exposures are rated using the approved methodology and reviewed at
least annually.

Operation: the control executes in the model inventory. Exceptions are routed to the Model
Risk Manager for disposition by the end of the following month.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the proportion of
reviews completed within the required period.
