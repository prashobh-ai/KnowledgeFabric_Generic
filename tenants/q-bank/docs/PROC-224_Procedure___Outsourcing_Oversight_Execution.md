# Procedure — Outsourcing Oversight Execution

**Document number:** PROC-224

## Procedure

1. Confirm the customer or exposure is in scope for Outsourcing Oversight.
2. Perform the assessment in the customer onboarding portal using the approved template.
3. Verify that alerts are dispositioned by a competent reviewer with documented rationale.
4. Where the standard is not met, escalate within five business days.
5. Record the outcome so that control CTL-502 can be evidenced.
