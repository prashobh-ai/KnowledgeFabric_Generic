# Control — Collateral Valuation (CTL-451)

## Control Objective and Operation

Control CTL-451 exists to satisfy obligation OBL-139 concerning Collateral Valuation.

Implements policy CPOL-017 and is executed through PROC-231.

Objective: ensure that identity is verified and the source of funds is understood before
onboarding.

Operation: the control executes in the credit origination workflow. Exceptions are routed to
the Financial Crime Lead for disposition within 24 hours of identification.

Testing: the control is tested on a sample basis; the reported measure is the proportion of
executions with complete evidence retained.
