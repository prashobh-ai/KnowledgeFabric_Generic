# Quality Procedure — Complaint Handling and Reporting

**Document number:** QMP-119  
**Document type:** Quality Management Procedure  
**Owner:** Clara Castellano (Manufacturing Quality Engineer, STF-6079)  
**Approved by:** Anita Trelawney (Manufacturing Quality Engineer, STF-4733)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-01  

## Procedure

1. Record every complaint concerning devices including the QM-100 Analyser on receipt, in
the design control repository.
2. Determine whether the complaint concerns a device malfunction or operation outside the
specified temperature range.
3. Assess reportability against 21 CFR 820.30.
4. Where reportable, submit within the required timeframe.
5. Trend complaints; where a trend emerges, raise a CAPA.

Owner: Manufacturing Quality Engineer. Oversight: Manufacturing Quality Engineer.
