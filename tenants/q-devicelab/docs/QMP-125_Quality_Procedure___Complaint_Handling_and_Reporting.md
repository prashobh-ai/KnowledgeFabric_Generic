# Quality Procedure — Complaint Handling and Reporting

**Document number:** QMP-125  
**Document type:** Quality Management Procedure  
**Owner:** Ravi Ferrers (Director of Regulatory Affairs, STF-2884)  
**Approved by:** Mei Solberg (Clinical Affairs Manager, STF-7932)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-07-07  

## Procedure

1. Record every complaint concerning devices including the QM-520 Point-of-Care Meter on
receipt, in the field action tracker.
2. Determine whether the complaint concerns a device malfunction or use of a consumable
beyond its expiry.
3. Assess reportability against 21 CFR 803.50.
4. Where reportable, submit within the required timeframe.
5. Trend complaints; where a trend emerges, raise a CAPA.

Owner: Director of Regulatory Affairs. Oversight: Clinical Affairs Manager.
