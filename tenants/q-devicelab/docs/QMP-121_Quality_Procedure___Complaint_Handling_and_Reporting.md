# Quality Procedure — Complaint Handling and Reporting

**Document number:** QMP-121  
**Document type:** Quality Management Procedure  
**Owner:** Lucia Ravensworth (Director of Regulatory Affairs, STF-8339)  
**Approved by:** Clara Okonjo (Manufacturing Quality Engineer, STF-8265)  
**Revision:** C  
**Effective:** 2026-03-03  

## Procedure

1. Record every complaint concerning devices including the QM-700 Surgical Navigation Unit
on receipt, in the post-market surveillance database.
2. Determine whether the complaint concerns a device malfunction or an interfering substance
above the stated concentration.
3. Assess reportability against 21 CFR 820.30.
4. Where reportable, submit within the required timeframe.
5. Trend complaints; where a trend emerges, raise a CAPA.

Owner: Director of Regulatory Affairs. Oversight: Manufacturing Quality Engineer.
