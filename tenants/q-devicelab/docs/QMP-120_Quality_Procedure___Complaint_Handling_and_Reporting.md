# Quality Procedure — Complaint Handling and Reporting

**Document number:** QMP-120  
**Document type:** Quality Management Procedure  
**Owner:** Idris Vasquez (Clinical Affairs Manager, STF-4496)  
**Approved by:** Idris Castellano (Manufacturing Quality Engineer, STF-6941)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-02-02  

## Procedure

1. Record every complaint concerning devices including the QM-450 Imaging Console on
receipt, in the post-market surveillance database.
2. Determine whether the complaint concerns a device malfunction or use of a consumable
beyond its expiry.
3. Assess reportability against ISO 13485 Clause 8.2.2.
4. Where reportable, submit within the required timeframe.
5. Trend complaints; where a trend emerges, raise a CAPA.

Owner: Clinical Affairs Manager. Oversight: Manufacturing Quality Engineer.
