## Hazard, Control and Residual Risk

Hazard analysis entry for the QM-100 Analyser.

Hazard: an incompletely seated cartridge. Foreseeable sequence of events: the condition is
not detected, and a result is acted upon that does not reflect the true value.

Risk control: the design detects and flags the condition; the instructions for use state the
limitation explicitly.

Residual risk is judged acceptable against the benefit of visualisation and review of
acquired diagnostic images. Verified through the design verification record.
