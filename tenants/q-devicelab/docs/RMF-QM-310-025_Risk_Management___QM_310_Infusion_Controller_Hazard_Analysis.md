## Hazard, Control and Residual Risk

Hazard analysis entry for the QM-310 Infusion Controller.

Hazard: an out-of-range ambient temperature. Foreseeable sequence of events: the condition
is not detected, and a result is acted upon that does not reflect the true value.

Risk control: the design detects and flags the condition; the instructions for use state the
limitation explicitly.

Residual risk is judged acceptable against the benefit of point-of-care determination for
professional use. Verified through the design verification record.
