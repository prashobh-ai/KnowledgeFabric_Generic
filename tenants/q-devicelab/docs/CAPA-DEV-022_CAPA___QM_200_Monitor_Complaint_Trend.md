## Root Cause and Action

CAPA raised on complaint CMP-2026-05026 and a trend of reports of an error code presented
during start-up for the QM-200 Monitor (cleared under K241137).

Root cause: the instructions for use did not state the limitation concerning an interfering
substance above the stated concentration prominently enough at the point of use.

Corrective action: revise the instructions for use and issue a customer notification.
Preventive action: add the limitation to the design review checklist.

Effectiveness verified through complaint rate monitoring.
