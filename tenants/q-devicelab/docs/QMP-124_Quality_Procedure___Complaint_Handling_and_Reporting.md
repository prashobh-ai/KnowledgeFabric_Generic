# Quality Procedure — Complaint Handling and Reporting

**Document number:** QMP-124  
**Document type:** Quality Management Procedure  
**Owner:** Pieter Castellano (Design Assurance Engineer, STF-3141)  
**Approved by:** Karl Solberg (Director of Regulatory Affairs, STF-5143)  
**Revision:** F  
**Effective:** 2026-06-06  

## Procedure

1. Record every complaint concerning devices including the QM-200 Monitor on receipt, in the
design control repository.
2. Determine whether the complaint concerns a device malfunction or an interfering substance
above the stated concentration.
3. Assess reportability against 21 CFR 820.30.
4. Where reportable, submit within the required timeframe.
5. Trend complaints; where a trend emerges, raise a CAPA.

Owner: Design Assurance Engineer. Oversight: Director of Regulatory Affairs.
