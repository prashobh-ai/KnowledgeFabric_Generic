# Task Card — Navigation Inspection — QA-737M

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to raise an Engineering Order and defer
under the applicable relief on the Navigation system (ATA Chapter 34) of aircraft QA-737M.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2024-0274 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2024-0477. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the maintenance information system.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the navigation installation for a repeat defect recorded on three consecutive
sectors.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Raise an engineering order and defer under the applicable relief.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under
EASA Part-CAMO CAMO.A.315, continuing airworthiness management.

Only Technical Records Controller holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify
this task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the
date of completion, recorded in the maintenance information system.
