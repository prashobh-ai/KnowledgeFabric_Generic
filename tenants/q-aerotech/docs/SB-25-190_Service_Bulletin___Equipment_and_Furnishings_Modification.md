## Planning Information

This service bulletin introduces a modification to the Equipment and Furnishings system (ATA
Chapter 25) to address corrosion beyond allowable limits at the fastener row.

Compliance is recommended at 12 calendar months. Where an airworthiness directive mandates
this bulletin, the directive compliance time takes precedence.

Embodiment on aircraft QA-350K is planned during the next scheduled input and is managed
through engineering order EO-2024-0480. Where mandated, see ADCR-2024-0274.
