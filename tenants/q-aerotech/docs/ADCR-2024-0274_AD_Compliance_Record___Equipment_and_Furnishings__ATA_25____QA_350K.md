## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of moisture ingress at the connector
backshell affecting the Equipment and Furnishings system (ATA Chapter 25) on aircraft of the
type operated by Q-AeroTech. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in
degraded system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-350K and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: carry out a repetitive inspection at the stated interval. The compliance
time is 500 flight hours or 300 cycles, whichever occurs first from the effective date of
this directive.

Under EASA Part-145 145.A.50, certification of maintenance and release to service. Operators
must record accomplishment in the reliability reporting database before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Design Liaison
Engineer.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-350K in the reliability reporting
database, cross-referenced to task card TC-25-0378 raised to perform the work. Where a
service bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-25-190.
