# Task Card — Auto Flight Inspection — QA-321N

**Document number:** TC-22-0377

**Effective:** 2024-04-04  

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to raise an Engineering Order and defer
under the applicable relief on the Auto Flight system (ATA Chapter 22) of aircraft QA-321N.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2024-0274 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2026-0479. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the airworthiness compliance register.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the auto flight installation for moisture ingress at the connector backshell.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Raise an engineering order and defer under the applicable relief.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under 14
CFR 121.367, maintenance, preventive maintenance and alterations programme.

Only Head of Airworthiness Review holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify
this task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the
date of completion, recorded in the airworthiness compliance register.
