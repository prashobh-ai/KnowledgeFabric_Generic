## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of chafing of the harness against a
structural former affecting the Pneumatic system (ATA Chapter 36) on aircraft of the type
operated by Q-AeroTech. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded
system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-738M and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: raise an Engineering Order and defer under the applicable relief. The
compliance time is 750 flight hours from the effective date of this directive.

Under EASA Part-M M.A.301, continuing airworthiness tasks. Operators must record
accomplishment in the flight operations documentation portal before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Design Liaison
Engineer.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-738M in the flight operations
documentation portal, cross-referenced to task card TC-36-0380 raised to perform the work.
Where a service bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-24-185.
