# Service Bulletin — Electrical Power Modification

**Document number:** SB-24-185  
**Document type:** Service Bulletin  
**Owner:** Anders Nordstrand (Certifying Staff (B1), STF-7585)  
**Approved by:** Omar Lindqvist (Design Liaison Engineer, STF-2747)

## Planning Information

This service bulletin introduces a modification to the Electrical Power system (ATA Chapter
24) to address chafing of the harness against a structural former.

Compliance is recommended at 750 flight hours. Where an airworthiness directive mandates
this bulletin, the directive compliance time takes precedence.

Embodiment on aircraft QA-738M is planned during the next scheduled input and is managed
through engineering order EO-2025-0475. Where mandated, see ADCR-2026-0276.
