# Task Card — Fuel Inspection — QA-E90R

**Document number:** TC-28-0374

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to quarantine the part and raise a
technical query with the design organisation on the Fuel system (ATA Chapter 28) of aircraft
QA-E90R.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2024-0277 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2026-0476. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the electronic technical log.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the fuel installation for chafing of the harness against a structural former.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design organisation.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under 14
CFR 43.9, content, form and disposition of maintenance records.

Only Line Maintenance Supervisor holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify
this task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the
date of completion, recorded in the electronic technical log.
