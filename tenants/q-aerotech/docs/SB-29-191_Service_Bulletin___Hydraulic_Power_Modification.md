# Service Bulletin — Hydraulic Power Modification

**Document number:** SB-29-191  
**Document type:** Service Bulletin  
**Owner:** Idris Marchetti (Line Maintenance Supervisor, STF-3567)  
**Approved by:** Karl Bertrand (Certifying Staff (B1), STF-6204)  
**Revision:** B  
**Effective:** 2025-08-08  

## Planning Information

This service bulletin introduces a modification to the Hydraulic Power system (ATA Chapter
29) to address chafing of the harness against a structural former.

Compliance is recommended at 1,000 flight cycles. Where an airworthiness directive mandates
this bulletin, the directive compliance time takes precedence.

Embodiment on aircraft QA-320N is planned during the next scheduled input and is managed
through engineering order EO-2024-0474. Where mandated, see ADCR-2025-0275.
