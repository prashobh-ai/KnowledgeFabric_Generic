# Task Card — Air Conditioning Inspection — QA-320N

**Document number:** TC-21-0386  
**Document type:** Maintenance Task Card  
**Owner:** Marcus Okonjo (Certifying Staff (B1), STF-8496)  
**Approved by:** Anders Rahimi (Continuing Airworthiness Manager, STF-4183)  
**Revision:** A  
**Effective:** 2024-01-13  

## Purpose and Applicability

This task card provides the instructions necessary to raise an Engineering Order and defer
under the applicable relief on the Air Conditioning system (ATA Chapter 21) of aircraft
QA-320N.

The task is raised to satisfy the compliance requirement recorded in ADCR-2025-0275 and is
referenced by engineering order EO-2024-0474. Work must not commence until the applicable
maintenance data has been confirmed as current in the electronic technical log.

## Procedure

1. Confirm the aircraft is electrically safe and access panels are removed in accordance
with the maintenance manual.
2. Inspect the air conditioning installation for an out-of-tolerance actuator response time.
3. Record all measurements on the worksheet. Any measurement outside the stated limits
requires a technical query.
4. Raise an engineering order and defer under the applicable relief.
5. Restore access, carry out an operational test, and confirm no leakage or abnormal
indication.
6. Complete the certification block and enter the work against the aircraft record.

## Certification and Release

Certification of this task constitutes a release to service for the work performed. Under 14
CFR 39.7, operating a product that does not comply with an applicable AD.

Only Certifying Staff (B1) holding the appropriate category authorisation may certify this
task. The certifying signature must be accompanied by the authorisation number and the date
of completion, recorded in the electronic technical log.
