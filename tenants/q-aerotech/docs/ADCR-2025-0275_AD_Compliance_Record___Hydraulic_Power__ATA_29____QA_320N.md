# AD Compliance Record — Hydraulic Power (ATA 29) — QA-320N

## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of corrosion beyond allowable limits at the
fastener row affecting the Hydraulic Power system (ATA Chapter 29) on aircraft of the type
operated by Q-AeroTech. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded
system function during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-320N and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: apply the approved repair scheme and record the deviation. The compliance
time is 12 calendar months from the effective date of this directive.

Under 14 CFR 91.417, maintenance records retention. Operators must record accomplishment in
the electronic technical log before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Head of Airworthiness
Review.

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-320N in the electronic technical
log, cross-referenced to task card TC-29-0379 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-21-184.
