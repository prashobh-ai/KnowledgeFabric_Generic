## Planning Information

This service bulletin introduces a modification to the Fuel system (ATA Chapter 28) to
address moisture ingress at the connector backshell.

Compliance is recommended at 500 flight hours or 300 cycles, whichever occurs first. Where
an airworthiness directive mandates this bulletin, the directive compliance time takes
precedence.

Embodiment on aircraft QA-E90R is planned during the next scheduled input and is managed
through engineering order EO-2026-0476. Where mandated, see ADCR-2024-0277.
