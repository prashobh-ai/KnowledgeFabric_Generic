## Unsafe Condition and Required Action

This directive is issued in response to reports of an out-of-tolerance actuator response
time affecting the Wings system (ATA Chapter 57) on aircraft of the type operated by
Q-AeroTech. The unsafe condition, if not addressed, could result in degraded system function
during flight.

Applicability extends to the aircraft identified by registration QA-E90R and to all aircraft
of the same type and configuration within the fleet.

Required action: quarantine the part and raise a technical query with the design
organisation. The compliance time is each transit check until terminating action is embodied
from the effective date of this directive.

Under 14 CFR 145.109, repair station equipment, materials and data requirements. Operators
must record accomplishment in the safety reporting system before further flight.

## Alternative Methods and Recording

No alternative method of compliance has been approved at the date of issue. Requests for an
alternative method must be submitted with supporting data through the Certifying Staff (B1).

Accomplishment must be recorded against registration QA-E90R in the safety reporting system,
cross-referenced to task card TC-28-0374 raised to perform the work. Where a service
bulletin provides the terminating action, refer to SB-28-186.
