## Defect and Rectification

Defect reported by the operating crew on aircraft QA-787J: moisture ingress at the connector
backshell associated with the Flight Controls system (ATA Chapter 27).

Initial assessment by Design Liaison Engineer confirmed the reported condition. The defect
was deferred under the applicable relief and an engineering order EO-2025-0478 was raised to
carry out a repetitive inspection at the stated interval.

Deferral applied under MEL-21-174. The entry remains open in the planning and forecasting
module and is reviewed at each transit check.
